## Standards

- **Attribution:** every framework is credited to the named author and primary work, not to this vault.
- **Grade:** all entries are `named-expert` — practitioner doctrine with strong industry consensus, not peer-reviewed RCTs. Do not elevate to T1 without independent evidence from `12-EVIDENCE`.
- **IP rule:** synthesis only — short quotes (< ~15 words), no passages, no chapter reproduction. Books stay in `Downloads/`, not in the repo.
- **Cross-module rule:** if a principle from these notes appears as a load-bearing claim in a page spec, it must cross-reference the relevant `12-EVIDENCE` bank or be flagged `named-expert` explicitly in the spec.
