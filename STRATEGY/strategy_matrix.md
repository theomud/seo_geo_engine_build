# Strategy Matrix

Use this before building content.

| Strategy | SEO Works? | GEO Works? | Better Channel | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reference data publisher | Yes | Yes | SEO/GEO | 10/10 |
| Lead-gen page | Yes | Partial | SEO + conversion | 7/10 |
| YouTube/video | Partial | Strong | Video + GEO | 8/10 |
| Community | Partial | Strong | Human participation | 7/10 |
| Direct B2B outreach | No | No | Phone, email, LinkedIn | 0/10 |

Rule: do not use SEO/GEO where direct sales would work better.
