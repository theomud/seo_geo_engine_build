# Lead-Generation Playbook (PRIMARY for this deployment)

> The end-to-end machine. Backed by `claim_bank.csv` (pillars `blog_purpose_business_model`
> and `lead_generation_content_system`). `blog_objective = lead_generation`.

## 0. The lens: what this blog is FOR

A blog is a different machine depending on its job. This deployment monetises **qualified
enquiries**, so it optimises for **funnel progression and cost-per-lead -- not pageviews.**
A post with 50,000 reads and zero quote requests has failed; 400 reads and 12 qualified
enquiries has succeeded. *(Numini objective; reconciled with content-strategy canon.)*

| Blog type | Goal | Primary metric | This deployment? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ad / affiliate | traffic | pageviews / RPM | NO |
| Lead-gen / service | qualified enquiries | leads & CPL | **YES** |

## 1. Intent -> funnel mapping

- **TOFU (informational):** "do I need to relocate my pet?" -> educate, capture, link down.
- **MOFU (commercial-investigation):** "best pet relocation Dubai to UK", costs, comparisons.
- **BOFU (transactional):** "pet relocation quote / book" -> money page.
- [ ] Every post is tagged to a stage **and** to the money page it feeds.

## 2. Money pages vs supporting content

- Route/service pages are the **conversion assets** (money pages).
- Blog posts are **demand capture** that internally link into the money pages (topical
  cluster). **Never leave an informational post as a dead end.**

## 3. Conversion-centered design (on the money page)

- [ ] **Attention ratio toward 1:1** -- one page, one goal; strip nav and competing links.
- [ ] **Message match** between the entry source (ad/search/post) and the page.
- [ ] **Single primary CTA.**
- [ ] **Friction-minimised form** (fewest fields) with trust signals beside it.
      *(Baymard form usability; conversion-centered design is Tier-2 expert consensus, MODERATE.)*

## 4. Lead capture & handoff

- [ ] Lead magnets / content upgrades / a price calculator where useful.
- [ ] Quote form -> **WhatsApp / Telegram bot handoff** as the qualification step.
- [ ] Exit-intent capture as a backstop.

## 5. Nurture

- [ ] Email list + sequence for leads not ready now -- the asset the traffic-era playbooks
      were right about. Build the list from day one.

## 6. Qualification & routing

- [ ] Bot qualifies -> score -> **fulfil or resell** the lead to a partner.

## 7. Measurement (leads, not traffic)

- [ ] Define conversion events; track **leads, CPL, funnel drop-off, lead quality, attribution.**
- [ ] Feed results into the `AB_Test_Protocol.md`.
- [ ] Tie to Numini **Engine 9 (Lead Generation)** patterns where they exist.

## Pitfalls

- Dead-end informational posts; cluttered conversion pages; quoting vendor uplift % as
  guarantees; measuring success by pageviews.
