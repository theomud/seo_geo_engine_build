# The 15 Principles — logic & voice doctrine

> **People do not care about your business. They care about themselves.** Everything flows from that.

The persuasion/voice doctrine the writer obeys and the auditor scores. Companion to

## The Master Formula
`WHO are we talking to? → WHAT do they want? → WHY don't they have it? → WHAT HAPPENS if nothing changes? → WHAT SHOULD THEY DO? → WHY SHOULD THEY TRUST US? → WHAT IS THE NEXT STEP?`

## The Ultimate Rule
> Before publishing anything, ask: **"If I were my perfect customer, would I feel understood within the first 10 seconds?"** If no, rewrite. The best copy never sounds like marketing — it sounds like someone accurately describing the reader's situation, then helping.
