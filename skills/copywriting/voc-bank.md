# Voice-of-Customer bank — UAE pet relocation

> Real customer **language** mined from expat forums, expat-voice blogs and competitor FAQs (Jun 2026).
> Use these *exact words* in copy — they convert because they're the reader's own (Joanna Wiebe).
> **These are language samples, not fact sources** — every factual claim still goes through the
> claim-audit + official-source screenshot. Sourced for provenance, not for truth.

## How to use
When writing, pull the reader's own phrasing from here for headlines, the fear-first hook, and FAQ
question wording. Match the question verbatim where it fits search intent. Feed into the writer's
Angle/Research step.

## Real questions (verbatim — use as FAQ headings / H2s)
- "Which airline is best for pets leaving UAE?"
- "Does Emirates SkyCargo accept pets from Dubai?"
- "Can pets transit through Doha or Istanbul?"
- "Are certain dog breeds banned from flying from the UAE?"
- "Do I need a rabies blood test (RNATT) for Europe or the UK?"
- "Can I use a sedative to keep my dog calm?"
- "Can I take my dog to / from Dubai?" · "How long does it take?" · "Will my pet be quarantined?"
*(source: MoveConnector FAQ; expat forums; search "people also ask")*

## Cost — how they talk about price
- "quoted 13,000 AED by a pet relocation specialist… we did it ourselves and it cost around 8,000 AED"
- "from 12,000 AED for a small dog to a … St Bernard being 33,000 AED"
- "Stop guessing — here's the realistic breakdown of what you'll pay"
- worry: being overcharged vs DIY "20–30% cheaper"
*(source: BritishMums; MoveConnector)*

## Timing & urgency (their words)
- "Don't wait until your visa is cancelled."
- "If you start late, you may have to leave your pet behind in boarding."
- "approval could take up to 48 hours" · "arrive at the DNATA export counter 4 hours before the flight"
- mental model: "1 month (US/UK/EU) to 7 months (Australia)"
*(source: MoveConnector; BritishMums)*

## Summer heat (a top fear, May–Sep)
- "daytime temperatures on the Dubai tarmac exceed 40°C"
- "airlines restrict pet travel when ground temperatures exceed safe limits"
- "you must book night flights or use climate-controlled transfers"
- snub-nosed/brachycephalic breeds: "increased risk of heat stroke" in summer
*(source: MoveConnector; BritishMums)*

## Breeds
- "Are certain dog breeds banned from flying from the UAE?"
- "Snub-nosed (brachycephalic) breeds like Bulldogs, Pugs, Boxers face seasonal or full restrictions — consult your agent before booking."

## Quarantine fear (the destination side)
- "UK authorities are extremely strict, and even minor errors can lead to a 21-day or longer quarantine."
- relief framing they respond to: "the UAE is a listed country, so no rabies blood test or 3-month wait" (for the UK).

## Emotional / trust language (mirror this tone)
- "Taking your pet back home when you move can sound **stressful** — but it doesn't have to be."
- reassurance that lands: "they'll guide you along the whole way."
- attachment words they use: **"back home"**, **"rescue dog"**, "our girl", "bring him home".
- DIY-risk they fear: "missed paperwork = rejected entry."

## Phrases worth owning in our copy
- "Stop guessing" (about price) · "no surprises at the border" · "every document, in order"
- "get your pet home safely" · "we'll guide you the whole way" · "the one detail that gets pets refused"
