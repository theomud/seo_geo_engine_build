# Truth Policy

## Non-negotiable rules

No invented statistics.
No invented citations.
No fake case studies.
No fake reviews.
No fake expertise.
No fake customer stories.
No exaggerated guarantees.
No unsupported claims.

## Every claim must be one of these

- verified by a source
- measured by us
- clearly marked as opinion
- clearly marked as assumption
- removed

## Evidence tiers

PRIMARY = own measurement or first-party data
T1 = sworn testimony, legal evidence, official regulatory/legal record
T2 = official documentation
T3 = verified leak or confirmed field existence, not confirmed weight
T4 = peer-reviewed paper or academic source
T5 = patent
T6 = practitioner consensus or market observation
UNKNOWN = not acceptable for factual publication without review

## Rule

If we cannot prove it, we do not publish it as fact.
