# Department Charter

## Mission

Build a disciplined SEO + GEO content engine that creates trust, authority, leads, and revenue through useful, evidence-based content.

## Success means

- Pages are useful to humans
- Pages are based on verified evidence
- Pages are optimized for search engines and AI answer engines
- Pages generate measurable business value
- The system improves after every execution

## Failure means

- Publishing content without evidence
- Publishing pages with no business purpose
- Chasing keywords without revenue logic
- Creating content that damages trust
- Measuring activity instead of results

## Responsibilities

The department must:

1. Decide what is worth creating
2. Research properly
3. Store reusable knowledge
4. Produce high-quality pages
5. Run QA before publishing
6. Track performance after publishing
7. Improve the engine continuously
