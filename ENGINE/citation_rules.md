# Citation Rules for GEO

Generative engines tend to extract passages, not whole pages. Pages should include clear, self-contained, quotable sections.

## Every important page should include

- clear definitions
- specific statistics where available
- cited sources where available
- short answer blocks
- FAQ blocks
- named entities
- updated dates for volatile information

## Do not do

- keyword stuffing
- fake citations
- fake quotes
- fake statistics
- hidden AI instructions
- AI-only markup hacks

## Citable passage format

Use short blocks like:

> Short factual answer: [answer]. Source: [source]. Last reviewed: [date].

## Minimum target for major pages

- 3 useful statistics or concrete facts
- 5 cited claims
- 5 self-contained passages
- 5 FAQs
- 1 clear definition section
