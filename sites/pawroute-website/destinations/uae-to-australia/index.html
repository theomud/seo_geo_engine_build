Quick answer

Moving a pet from Dubai to Australia is the world's strictest route and takes up to 6 months end to end, costing roughly AED 25,000–35,000+. It requires a rabies titre (RNATT) test, a 180-day wait after the test sample reaches the lab, an import permit, and quarantine on arrival (minimum 10 days with an identity check, otherwise 30).

Why Australia takes months, not weeks

Australia requires a rabies titer (RNAT) test at an approved lab, then a mandatory waiting period after you pass, and finally a 10-day government quarantine when your pet lands. Miss the sequence and the clock resets. This is why we map your exact dates before anything else.

What it takes

The Australia requirements

These are the headline steps. We confirm the current detail with the Australian Department of Agriculture for your specific pet and dates before you commit to anything.

ISO microchip first 15-digit 11784/11785 chip, implanted before any vaccination.
Rabies vaccine + titer test A rabies neutralising antibody titer test at an approved laboratory.
180-day waiting period Your pet can't be exported to Australia for 180 days after the RNATT sample reaches the lab — this is what makes the route take months. (It's a wait, not quarantine; your pet stays with you.)
Import permit Issued by the Australian Department of Agriculture (DAFF) before travel; assessment typically takes 10–20 business days.
Quarantine on arrival Minimum 10 days at a government facility if the optional identity check is done beforehand — otherwise a minimum of 30 days. Non-negotiable.

Dubai → Australia: your questions

Why does Australia take so long?
Because of the rabies titre (RNATT) test and the 180-day waiting period that follows it, plus quarantine on arrival. If you're moving in 6–12 months, start now.
How long is quarantine?
A minimum of 10 days if the optional identity check is completed before the RNATT, otherwise a minimum of 30 days. It's a government requirement at an approved facility — every pet goes through it.
What happens if a step is done out of order?
On this route, an out-of-order step (for example vaccinating before microchipping) can reset the clock and cost you months. That's exactly what the service is for — getting the sequence right.
Can you guarantee the arrival date?
No — and be wary of anyone who does. Airlines and quarantine slots move. We give you a realistic timeline and keep you updated, but we never guarantee a delivery date.
