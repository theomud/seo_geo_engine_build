Quick answer

Moving a pet from Dubai to the Netherlands follows the EU import framework — no quarantine, and often the most affordable European route at roughly AED 10,000–16,000. You'll need a microchip, a rabies vaccine given 21+ days before travel and an EU animal health certificate. Important: pets entering the EU from a non-listed country can also need a rabies antibody (titre) test, taken at least 30 days after vaccination and 90+ days before travel — we confirm whether the UAE requires it, because it can add months to the timeline.

Check the rabies titre test early

EU rules require a rabies antibody (titre) test for pets arriving from non-listed countries. Whether the UAE counts as listed decides if your pet needs it — and because the blood sample must be taken 90+ days before travel, it's the one thing that can turn a 4–8 week move into a multi-month one. Dubai → Netherlands: your questions

Is there quarantine for the Netherlands?
No quarantine when your pet meets the EU rules and the paperwork is correct.
Why is it cheaper than some EU routes?
Amsterdam Schiphol is a major, pet-friendly cargo hub with frequent Dubai connections, which helps keep freight costs competitive.
Will my pet need a rabies titre test?
It depends on whether the UAE is an EU-listed country. Pets from non-listed countries need a rabies antibody (titre) test taken at least 30 days after vaccination and 90+ days before travel. We confirm your status against the official EU guidance before you commit, because it materially changes the timeline.
How long does it take?
Usually 4–8 weeks when no titre test is required — driven mainly by the 21-day minimum after the rabies vaccine and cargo availability. If a titre test is needed, allow several months.
Can you guarantee the arrival date?
No. Airlines change schedules. We give you a realistic timeline and keep you updated, but never guarantee a delivery date.
