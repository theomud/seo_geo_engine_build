Quick answer

Moving a pet from Dubai to Canada takes about 4–6 weeks and costs roughly AED 14,000–20,000, with no quarantine when the CFIA requirements are met. You'll need a microchip, a current rabies vaccination and a health certificate. Some breed rules are set by province rather than nationally, so we confirm both for your destination before booking.

Dubai → Canada: your questions

Is there quarantine for Canada?
No quarantine when your pet meets the CFIA requirements and the paperwork is correct.
Do I need an import permit?
Canada doesn't require a pre-import permit for pet cats and dogs, but CFIA conditions must be satisfied on arrival. We confirm the current detail for your case.
Are some breeds restricted?
Breed rules can be provincial rather than national. We verify the rules for your specific destination before booking.
Can you guarantee the arrival date?
No. Airlines change schedules. We give you a realistic timeline and keep you updated, but never guarantee a delivery date.
