Moving your pet to Singapore?

Singapore is strict — close to Australia in difficulty. There's a titer test, an import licence, a 90-day wait and quarantine on arrival. It's very doable, but only if you start early and do every step in order.

Quick answer

Moving a pet from Dubai to Singapore takes about 4–8 months and costs roughly AED 18,000–28,000. It needs an RNATT rabies titre test (≥0.5 IU/ml), an AVS import licence, a 90-day wait after the titre passes, and 30 days of quarantine at the Sembawang facility on arrival.

What it takes

The Singapore requirements

The headline steps below come from Singapore's Animal & Veterinary Service (AVS). We confirm the current detail for your pet and dates before you commit.

ISO microchip first 15-digit chip, implanted before vaccination.
RNATT titer test Rabies Neutralising Antibody Titre Test — must read at least 0.5 IU/ml.
90-day wait After the titer passes — less strict than Australia's 180 days, but still months.
AVS import licence Required from Singapore's Animal & Veterinary Service before travel.
30-day quarantine On arrival, at the Sembawang quarantine facility.

Dubai → Singapore: your questions

What's the RNATT test?
The Rabies Neutralising Antibody Titre Test. Your pet's result must be at least 0.5 IU/ml, and it sets off a 90-day waiting period before travel.
How long is quarantine?
30 days at the Sembawang facility. It's a government requirement.
Is Singapore as hard as Australia?
Close. The 90-day wait is shorter than Australia's 180 days, but the licence, titer test and quarantine make it one of the strictest routes.
Can you guarantee the arrival date?
No. Airline and quarantine slots change. We give you a realistic timeline and keep you informed, but we never guarantee a delivery date.
