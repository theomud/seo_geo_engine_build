Quick answer

Moving a pet from Dubai to the UK takes about 4–8 weeks and costs roughly AED 12,000–18,000 for a dog (AED 8,000–14,000 for a cat). There is no quarantine when the paperwork is correct — but dogs must have a tapeworm treatment 1–5 days before arrival, and the health certificate must come from an APHA-approved vet.

The #1 mistake on the UK route

Dogs need a tapeworm treatment 1–5 days before arrival in the UK — not earlier, not later. Too early and it's invalid; too late and your dog is refused entry or quarantined. Cats don't need it. We schedule this around your flight so the window is never missed.

What you'll need

Requirements for the UK

Two sides to satisfy: the UAE export rules (MOCCAE) and the UK import rules (APHA). We handle both.

ISO microchip 11784/11785, 15-digit — implanted before the rabies vaccine, or the vaccine is invalid.
Rabies vaccine At least 21 days before departure, with batch numbers on the certificate.
APHA-approved vet The UK health certificate must come from a vet on APHA's approved list. Most Dubai vets aren't — DKC and British Vet Hospital are. We verify before booking.
Export health certificate Issued by a government vet, valid 30 days — and it must still be valid at arrival, not just departure.
MOCCAE export clearance Online application plus an airport cargo-terminal inspection.
Tapeworm treatment (dogs only) 1–5 days before UK arrival. Cats are exempt.

Dubai → UK: your questions

Do I need a UK import permit?
No. The UK doesn't issue pre-import permits for pets. You just need the APHA health certificate.
Why can't I use my local Dubai vet?
APHA keeps a whitelist of approved third-country vets, and most Dubai vets aren't on it. DKC is; British Vet Hospital is. We verify approval before any appointment.
What if I miss the tapeworm treatment timing?
Entry is blocked and your dog is quarantined or returned. It's the most common UK-route mistake, which is why we schedule it precisely against your flight.
Does my cat need the tapeworm treatment?
No. Cats are exempt for the UK. Only dogs need it.
What if the health certificate expires during transport?
It must be valid at arrival, not just departure. The certificate lasts 30 days from issue, so we coordinate the issue date with your cargo booking.
Can my Pit Bull go to the UK?
No. Pit Bull Terrier, Japanese Tosa, Dogo Argentino and Fila Brasileiro are banned under the Dangerous Dogs Act 1991 — and since 1 February 2024 the XL Bully is banned too (it can't be brought into the UK). Dogs are judged on physical type, not pedigree, so bully-type dogs can face extra scrutiny at entry. We confirm your breed before booking.
Will my pet be okay in cargo for 8+ hours?
A healthy pet travels safely in modern pressurised, temperature-controlled cargo holds. For pets over 10 years old we recommend a fit-to-fly check first. We never give medical advice — that's between you and your vet.
Do I need pet insurance?
It's not required, but recommended. Airline liability is limited (around AED 1,000).
What's the arrival process in the UK?
Your pet clears customs, usually within 30–60 minutes, then comes to you or a pet hotel. No quarantine when the paperwork is correct.
How much is UK customs clearance?
Usually AED 500–1,000 with correct documents. It can exceed AED 5,000 if paperwork is wrong, because of delays and re-inspections.
