Simple, in the right order

How your pet's relocation works

Most mistakes happen because steps are done out of order. Here's the sequence that works.

Tell us the destination & date

We confirm what your specific route needs and flag anything urgent — like summer heat embargoes or long titer-test waits.

Microchip, then vaccinate

ISO microchip first, then the rabies vaccine. Order matters — vaccinating before microchipping makes it invalid.

Paperwork & MOCCAE export

Export health certificate (valid 30 days) plus the MOCCAE application and airport inspection.

Book the right crate & flight

IATA-compliant crate sized to your pet, and cargo space booked early — it fills 30–60 days ahead.

Fly & clear customs

Your pet travels in a temperature-controlled hold and clears customs at the other end. We coordinate the handover.
