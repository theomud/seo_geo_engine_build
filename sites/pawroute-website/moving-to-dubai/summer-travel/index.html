Moving to/from Dubai · timing

Can I fly my pet in the Dubai summer?

This is the catch that surprises people most. From roughly May to September, airlines restrict or refuse pet cargo when it's too hot — for your pet's safety. It doesn't make a move impossible; it means you plan around it. Here's how.

How to plan around it Ask about your dates
🐾✈️

Quick answer

Many airlines restrict or ban pet cargo when ground temperatures exceed about 30°C, which in the UAE means high risk from roughly May to September — peaking June to August. A summer move isn't impossible, but it can be delayed by weeks, so timing and airline choice matter. We never put a pet on a flight that isn't safe for the conditions.

  • High-risk monthsMay–September
  • Typical triggerGround temp > 30°C
  • ImpactDelays, limited flights
  • Delivery datesNever guaranteed

How to plan around the heat embargo

Start early and stay flexible on dates

Build slack into your timeline so a heat delay doesn't derail your move.

Choose airlines and routes that handle summer pets

Some carriers and routings have temperature-controlled handling and cooler transit hubs. We match you to one.

Get an honest go / no-go call

If conditions aren't safe, we say so. Your pet only flies when it's safe — no exceptions.

Why we won't promise a date in summer

Airlines change or cancel pet cargo at short notice when temperatures spike. We give you a realistic plan and keep you updated — but we never guarantee a summer delivery date, and we never give veterinary advice about heat fitness. That's a conversation for you and your vet.
