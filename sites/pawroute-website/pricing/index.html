Why volumetric weight matters

Airlines charge by actual weight or crate volume — whichever is higher. A crate of 80 × 60 × 60 cm counts as about 48 kg even if your dog is 20 kg. Sizing the crate correctly is part of what we do, so you're not surprised at the cargo desk.

Pricing questions

Why do you show prices when competitors don't?
