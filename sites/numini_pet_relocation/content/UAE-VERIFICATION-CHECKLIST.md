## ✅ VERIFIED 9 Jun 2026 — official MOCCAE pet page (verbatim)
Screenshots: `audit/evidence/uae-import-export/moccae-pets.png` + `moccae-pets-full.png`. The UAE
import side is now almost fully official-sourced:
- **Permits:** import permit **valid 90 days**; export permit **valid 30 days** (two different permits — the earlier "conflict"). Can't import on an expired permit.
- **Microchip:** ✅ a **permanent microchip is required**, and the chip number on the health certificate **must match** the implanted chip.
- **Rabies window:** ✅ for a first/lapsed vaccination, **≥21 days** must pass after the rabies shot; for a valid booster, no 21-day wait. Vaccinate at age **≥12 weeks**.
- **Titre (high-risk countries only):** rabies antibody titration **≥0.5 IU/ml**, from an accredited lab, certificate valid **365 days** (while the vaccine stays valid). May be recorded in the health certificate.
- **Min age to import:** **12 weeks from low-risk** countries; **≥15 weeks from high-risk** countries.
- **Parasite treatment:** internal + external parasite doses within **14 days before shipment**, noted on the cert/passport.
- **Max pets:** **2 per person per year** (2 cats, or 2 dogs, or 1+1) — except resident pets returning.
- **At release:** customs declaration/bill of lading + **vaccination doc or passport** (with chip no., description, vaccine details) + a **certified veterinary health certificate** from the origin authority.
- **Transport:** per **IATA**; shipped directly, no mixing with other pets in transit.
- **Fees:** import permit **AED 200/head**; release **AED 500/dog**, **AED 250/cat**.
- **Service/ESA/medical dogs:** extra docs (training cert, owner medical report, ownership pledge).
- **Special:** Bengal & Serval cats need a **5th-generation pedigree certificate**.
- **Entry/release:** veterinary-quarantine centres at approved ports; release permit issued electronically.

**Still to confirm — only one item left:** the **specific banned-breed list** (A-7). MOCCAE names the
legal basis — **Federal Law 22/2016 (Possession of Dangerous Animals)** + Decree 335/2018 — but the
actual breed list must come from that law's executive regulations + **Dubai Municipality**'s list.
