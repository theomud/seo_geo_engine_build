# Proprietary Operational Knowledge We Already Have

> **The GEO thesis.** AI engines cite content that contains operational knowledge unavailable
> elsewhere — lived patterns, route friction, real numbers, observed airline behaviour. We
> already hold a lot of this, mined from real Reddit threads and Facebook groups (69 screenshots,
> dated 2026-05-26/27). It is currently buried in research notes. This file inventories it so it
> can be moved into pages.

## Truth policy (read first)

This is **community-observed intelligence**, not our own service record. We have not yet served
clients, so we have **no proprietary stats of our own** (no "X% of moves", no "average delay we
saw"). Every nugget below is framed as what real owners reported in public expat forums — which
is *itself* a defensible, citable, GEO-elite source as long as we attribute it honestly. The
moat is not "we did 500 moves"; the moat is **"we systematically read every Dubai pet thread and
synthesised what actually happens, then checked it against the official MOCCAE page."** No
competitor has done this (Competitor file: 0 of 9 scored competitors address the confiscation
fear, the Sharjah question, or titer cost+timeline).

### Tag key
- **PUBLISHABLE-NOW** — defensible if framed honestly as community/observed intelligence (and/or
  backed by a Verified official source). Safe to put in content today.
- **NEEDS-VERIFICATION** — real and reported, but a specific number/claim conflicts with or is
  absent from the official source. Route to claim-audit; publish only as hedged community report
  until resolved.
- **SENSITIVE** — do not publish as-is (names a private individual, or trauma we shouldn't
  exploit). Use the *pattern*, not the person.

### What the official-source check already settled (the C-IDs)
The Source Bank (skill-official-source-research/SOURCE-INDEX.md, captured 2026-05-28) already
verified or contradicted several community numbers. This is gold for GEO because it lets us
publish with *"community says X; the official MOCCAE/airline page says Y"* — a level of honesty
competitors never reach.
- **Verified true:** 500 AED dog / 250 AED cat release fee (C-003); rabies vaccine valid only
  ≥21 days after first shot (C-007); MOCCAE import permit valid 90 days (C-010); permit applied

## THEME 1 — Airlines (real observed behaviour, carrier by carrier)

### 1.1 Etihad is the documented worst case for flying a pet [NEEDS-VERIFICATION on the $ figure; PUBLISHABLE-NOW as a community pattern]
- **Nugget:** Across community reports, Etihad is consistently the most expensive, most
  stressful, and most likely to leave a pet's approval to the last minute. One first-person
  Abu Dhabi→Canada cabin account (r/UAE, unnnabear, 7 upvotes): emailed Mar 8 for an Apr 3
  flight, paid ~5,000 AED for an extra seat + 5,500 AED pet fee *before* documents were
  accepted, was wrongly asked for an EU Health Certificate on a **Canada** route that doesn't
  need one, and received final approval at 9:56 PM — **4.5 hours before a 2 AM flight.**
  "I felt like they took advantage of the desperate circumstances." / "I WOULD NEVER FLY
  EITHAD if there are other options available."
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings.md Batch 3; SCREENSHOT-EVIDENCE Batch 3 (files
  1000305418–423).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW as an attributed community account (the *pattern* and the *timeline*).
  **NEEDS-VERIFICATION** on the specific "$1,500 cabin fee" history — the official Etihad page
  showed a USD 399 promo, not 1,500 (C-015). Publish as: "community reports a fee history rising
  to ~$1,500; Etihad's own page currently shows $399 — always check the operator's live page."

### 1.2 Turkish Airlines and Lufthansa are the community's preferred alternatives [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** When owners complain about Etihad, the same two carriers are repeatedly named as
  "way more accommodating and responsive": **Turkish Airlines** and **Lufthansa**. Turkish in
  particular is used to keep pets *in cabin* by routing Dubai→Turkey→Serbia (transit trick).
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings.md Batches 1 & 3 (ciceroblues; Mairuru, 8 upvotes;
  Sammy12xyz). Profile §16.
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW (attributed community preference; not a numeric claim).

### 1.3 Royal Jordanian: cabin pet for ~$100 from Dubai — backed by the official page [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** Owners flew dogs/cats **in cabin** from Dubai Airport on Royal Jordanian for
  ~$100 USD with minimal docs (vaccinations + passport + export permit + government health cert).
  This is one of the few community numbers the official source *confirms*: Royal Jordanian's own
  pets page publishes small-pet Middle-East cabin (PETC) pricing in the ~USD 85–100 range.
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings.md Batch 4 (juvegy thread, 22 upvotes); SOURCE-INDEX
  C-016 (Verified).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW — community report **and** verified official source agree. Strongest
  airline nugget we have.

### 1.4 flydubai and Hi Fly do NOT take cabin pets [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** flydubai is **cargo-only** — no animals in the cabin (official, verified). Hi Fly
  takes no pets at all; in one report Etihad replaced a cancelled flight with a Hi Fly aircraft,
  which stranded a pet and forced the owner onto a private jet (K9 Jets).
- **Provenance:** flydubai — SOURCE-INDEX C-022 (Verified). Hi Fly — facebook-group-findings.md
  Batch 5; Competitor file "negative competitive intelligence."
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW (flydubai is officially verified; Hi Fly framed as community report).

### 1.5 Emirates carries pets in the hold, not the cabin (except service dogs) [PUBLISHABLE-NOW — but correct the breed framing]
- **Nugget:** Community said "Emirates rejected my BSH cat" and "Emirates cabin = service dogs
  only." The official Emirates page confirms the *structure* — pets fly as excess baggage in the
  hold, no cabin carriage except service dogs — but **names no breed** and does not mention BSH.
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings.md (Pet Moving group; "I asked Emirates… they don't
  accept BSH"); SOURCE-INDEX C-013, C-021, C-023 (all Unverifiable on the breed/cabin specifics).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW for the verified structure (hold, not cabin; service dogs excepted).
  **NEEDS-VERIFICATION** for the "BSH rejected" claim — publish only as "an owner reported being
  told their British Shorthair wasn't accepted; Emirates' published page names no breed — confirm
  with the airline for your specific cat."

### 1.6 Air Cairo "just show up" is wrong per the official page [NEEDS-VERIFICATION → publish the correction]
- **Nugget:** Community said you can "just show up at the airport with documentation" on Air
  Cairo. The official Air Cairo pets page **contradicts** this: pets must be registered in
  advance via the call centre, "Pets that have not been registered in advance may not be
  accepted."
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings.md Batch 4; SOURCE-INDEX C-017 (Unverifiable —
  contradicted).
- **Tag:** NEEDS-VERIFICATION as a community claim — but this is a *great* GEO nugget published
  as a myth-correction: "A popular forum tip says you can just show up on Air Cairo — their own
  page says the opposite. Register in advance."

### 1.7 Excess baggage is cheaper than manifest cargo [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** Repeated across batches — flying a pet as **excess/accompanied baggage** is much
  cheaper than booking it as manifest cargo (one Spain route owner: dog flew as excess baggage,
  "much cheaper than flying as cargo").
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings.md Batch 1 (gulp_daddy); "Practical Hacks" list item 3.
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW (attributed community pattern, qualitative not numeric).

## THEME 2 — Airports (the route-experience moat)

### 2.1 The Sharjah airport advantage [PUBLISHABLE-NOW as community intel; NEEDS-VERIFICATION on "20 min"]
- **Nugget:** The single highest-value practical insight in the dataset. Owners report Sharjah
  (SHJ) as the simplest, cheapest UAE departure for flying with a pet: more airlines allow cabin
  pets, no pre-flight document back-and-forth with the airline, and the airport process finishes
  fast — one owner said "I finished the process at Sharjah airport in 20 minutes."
  **Three-airport ranking the community converges on: Sharjah (best) > Dubai (Royal Jordanian,
  $100) > Abu Dhabi / Etihad ($1,500+, worst).**
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings.md Batch 4 (juvegy, 22 upvotes); Profile §16
  ("Sharjah airport hack").
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW as attributed community intelligence and a comparison framing.
  **NEEDS-VERIFICATION** on the literal "20 minutes" (not on any official page — C-018) and on
  the regulatory point: MOCCAE rules apply identically at all three airports, so the honest page
  (already drafted) says *the regulator's requirements are the same everywhere; the route
  experience is what differs.* That honesty is the GEO win.

### 2.2 What's identical at all three airports vs what differs [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** The MOCCAE import permit (online, 90-day validity) applies to **every** UAE entry
  point — a "shortcut airport" never bypasses the permit or the rabies timeline. What differs is
  carrier choice, cabin availability, cost, and queue/processing experience.
- **Provenance:** content-structure template `sharjah-vs-dubai-vs-abu-dhabi…md`; SOURCE-INDEX
  C-019, C-010 (Verified).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW (verified official + honest hedge already worked out).

## THEME 3 — Timing & process (where the delays really come from)

### 3.1 The most common real failure point is titer-test *timing*, not the test itself [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** The defensible, un-fakeable operational lesson: the rabies titer test isn't the
  risk — its **timing read against the permit window** is. The quoted lab turnaround is often not
  the real one; the margin lives in the gap between the test date and the permit's validity, and
  a document review ~4 days out is what actually catches it. Owners report titer timelines that
  slip and "endless" quotes with no transparency.
- **Provenance:** skill-authority-assets case-study-airport-confiscation.md (composite,
  honestly labelled, built on Muze Gu + 7Ssisi reports + C-001/C-003/C-010/C-019).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW — this is our strongest *operational* (not just numeric) nugget. It
  reads as knowledge only someone who does this work would have, and it's built on verified
  official facts + honestly-labelled community reports.

### 3.2 Real route lead times owners reported [PUBLISHABLE-NOW as community ranges]
- **Nugget:** Owner-reported end-to-end timelines: **US/UK/EU ≈ 1 month**; **Australia ≈ 8
  months** ("sooo many dollars", via DKC); **Spain < 1 month** (Pet Express, excess baggage);
  a DIY mover took **~4 months** doing it himself (Dubai→Turkey→Serbia on Turkish).
- **Provenance:** voc-bank.md ("1 month US/UK/EU to 7 months Australia"); facebook-group-findings
  Batch 1 (mldl, gulp_daddy, Mairuru).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW as community-reported ranges (frame as "owners report", never as our
  measured averages). The Australia-is-8-months point is especially citable.

### 3.3 MOCCAE export is fully online and fast — but vaccine timing gates everything [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** The MOCCAE export/permit step is fully online and can be done in a day; the real
  constraint is **vaccine timing**, not the paperwork. Verified: permit applied online (C-019),
  valid 90 days (C-010), rabies valid only ≥21 days after the first shot (C-007), 500 AED dog /
  250 AED cat release fee if held (C-003).
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings Batches 2/4 (unnnabear, MOCCAE vet release 500 AED,
  1–2 days before); SOURCE-INDEX C-019/C-010/C-007/C-003 (Verified).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW (officially verified — publish with confidence).

### 3.4 Community process numbers that need the hedge [NEEDS-VERIFICATION]
- **Nugget:** Frequently repeated but **not** on the official MOCCAE page: Cargo Village health
  certificate valid **10 days** (C-009, absent); vaccines "30+ days" before travel (C-008 — the
  official figure is **21**, not 30); titer **2–3 weeks, only one UAE lab** (C-005/C-006, absent);
  MOCCAE vet release **500 AED paid 1–2 days before** (release-fee amount IS verified at C-003;
  the "1–2 days before" timing is community-sourced).
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings Batches 2/4/5; SOURCE-INDEX C-005, C-006, C-008, C-009.
- **Tag:** NEEDS-VERIFICATION — publish only as "community reports X; the official page does not
  state this / states 21 days." The 30-vs-21 correction is itself a citable accuracy win.

### 3.5 Book the pet with the airline before you book your own ticket [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** Operational sequencing tip the community learned the hard way: secure the pet's
  airline approval *first* — even four weeks out, Etihad only approved 4.5 hours before the
  flight. Book the pet before your own seat.
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings "Practical Hacks" item 4; Batch 3 account.
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW (process advice, no contested number).

## THEME 4 — Breeds & heat

### 4.1 Summer heat embargo (June–Sep) is an airline rule, not a MOCCAE rule [PUBLISHABLE-NOW with the honest split]
- **Nugget:** A "summer ban" on flying pets exists, but it's a **cargo/IATA temperature rule**,
  not a government rule — owners discover it *after* booking and scramble. Dubai tarmac exceeds
  40°C; the commonly-cited window is June–September; the fix is night flights / shoulder dates.
  MOCCAE requirements don't change by season.
- **Provenance:** voc-bank.md (summer heat section); Profile §16; content-structure template
  `can-i-move-my-pet-to-dubai-in-summer.md`.
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW with the verified/hedged split already worked out (MOCCAE season-
  neutral = verified C-019/C-010; exact embargo dates = airline/community, hedged). The
  *distinction itself* (regulator vs carrier) is the citable insight.

### 4.2 Brachycephalic (snub-nosed) breeds face heightened/seasonal restrictions [PUBLISHABLE-NOW as general; NEEDS-VERIFICATION for Emirates-specific]
- **Nugget:** Snub-nosed breeds (Bulldogs, Pugs, Boxers, and snub-nosed cats) carry increased
  heat-stroke risk and face seasonal or full airline restrictions — "consult your agent before
  booking."
- **Provenance:** voc-bank.md (breeds section).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW as a general, well-established industry fact. **NEEDS-VERIFICATION**
  if attributing a specific brachycephalic restriction to Emirates by name — the Emirates page we
  captured doesn't state it (C-023).

### 4.3 Destination breed bans are real and route-specific [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** Some destinations ban specific breeds outright. Verified examples: **UK** bans
  certain dog breeds unless the dog holds a Certificate of Exemption (C-027); **France** bans
  Category-1 dogs and conditions Category-2, and requires an import permit for Bengal/Savannah
  cats (C-059/C-062). This is route intelligence competitors don't surface.
- **Provenance:** SOURCE-INDEX C-027 (UK, Verified), C-059/C-062 (France, Verified).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW (officially verified per destination).

## THEME 6 — Fears (the emotional intelligence competitors ignore)

### 6.1 Confiscation fear: "taken at the airport and never given back" [PUBLISHABLE-NOW as pattern; SENSITIVE on the name]
- **Nugget:** The deepest fear in the entire dataset — the dog held at the airport and not
  returned. No competitor (0 of 9 scored) addresses it. It is well-founded: a pet presented
  without valid documentation can be held, with a 500 AED release fee (C-003) and a 90-day permit
  clock (C-010). This is the single highest-emotional-altitude page on the site and our ICP's
  defining fear.
- **Provenance:** Profile §5; facebook-group-findings Batch 2 (Muze Gu quote); ICP doc; Competitor
  file (gap 9/9).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW as a *pattern* ("owners in UAE pet groups describe this fear
  repeatedly"). **SENSITIVE** on the verbatim quote — Muze Gu is a private individual. Use the
  fear and her exact words anonymised/attributed-as-"one owner", as the existing fear-resolution
  template already does, rather than spotlighting the person.

### 6.2 The COVID-era "kept for research if positive" trauma [SENSITIVE]
- **Nugget:** A Stockholm→Dubai owner was made to sign that if the cat tested COVID-positive it
  would be kept for scientific research ("the trauma is real"). An extreme, real account.
- **Provenance:** Profile §5; facebook-group-findings Batch 4 (tantawi, 6 upvotes).
- **Tag:** SENSITIVE — do not feature. It's a pandemic-specific edge case tied to a named person
  and could read as fear-mongering. Keep out of published content; useful only as internal
  context for how acute the confiscation fear runs.

### 6.3 The UAE pet-abandonment crisis is real and current [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** Owners describe a current, real abandonment problem — pets left behind when expats
  leave ("uae already has a huge problem with abandoned pets that has skyrocketed"). This makes
  the PAA "Are people leaving pets behind in Dubai?" a genuine, answerable query and supports a
  "why we help pets leave safely" angle.
- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings Batch 5 (IrbisKat, 24 upvotes).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW as an attributed community observation (don't quantify it ourselves).

### 6.4 The discovery gap: owners don't know agencies exist [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** Real quote: "i never even knew such organisations existed." A meaningful share of
  the market doesn't know professional pet relocation is a service — an education-first content

- **Provenance:** facebook-group-findings Batch 1 (JustAlyna, OP).
- **Tag:** PUBLISHABLE-NOW (attributed quote; positioning insight).

### 6.5 Underserved segments competitors ignore [PUBLISHABLE-NOW]
- **Nugget:** Clear, repeatedly-asked gaps with no good answers anywhere: **bird relocation**
