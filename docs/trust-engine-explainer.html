How it fits together

The Trust Engine is the brain. Our engine is the hands.

The Trust Engine figures out what to build and verifies every fact. Our block renderer turns that into real pages. Then the measurement engines grade the result and decide what's next — a loop. Here's the whole pipeline in three steps.

1

The Brain

Decides & verifies · 9 engines + 1 gate

Reads the customer's fear, picks the page, and proves every claim against an official source — before a word is written.

Customer Fear Intelligence→ page + hero
Official Source Research→ sources
Content Structure→ block order
Conversion Copy→ cta + form
AI Citation (GEO)→ key_facts
Content Architecture→ nav + links
Trust Gap Analysis→ comparison
Authority Assets→ case_study*
Visual Evidence→ figure*
Editorial Judgment✓ QC gate
2

The Renderer — our engine

Builds the actual website

Takes the verified page data and assembles it into a fast, SEO + GEO-ready page — automatically adding schema, sitemap and the trust elements.

page YAML → blocks → HTML

herokey_factschecklist stepspricingsources faqctaJSON-LDsitemap
3

The Eyes

Measures & improves · 2 engines

Grades the live pages, watches real traffic, and tells the brain which page to build or fix next.

Website Audit→ grades pages
Content Intelligence Monitor→ what's next
↩ feedback loop — what to build next goes back to the Brain

↔ Side branch — Email Nurture

Fires after a lead fills the form. It's part of the funnel, but it isn't rendered into a page.

🛠 Meta tooling — Prompt Engineering · Skill Auditor

These build and quality-check the engines themselves, not the website. They keep the other 13 honest.

The takeaway: 10 engines feed into the renderer, 2 grade what comes out, 3 sit to the side — and the renderer (step 2) is the piece the Trust Engine never had.   * case_study and figure are two small new blocks we'd add to absorb Authority Assets & Visual Evidence.
