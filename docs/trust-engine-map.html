The 15 Trust Engine engines → our block renderer

Everything points at the hub. 10 engines feed page data into the renderer, 2 grade what comes out, and 3 sit off to the side. Our block-based engine is the render stage the Trust Engine was missing.

Upstream — feeds page data (×9) Quality gate (×1) Downstream — grades output (×2) Side branch (×1) Meta — builds the engines (×2)

Reading it: arrows into the hub = engines that produce page data (which page, the fear-led hero, cited sources, block order, CTAs, GEO passages). Arrows out = engines that consume what we publish (audit + monitoring loop). Dotted = meta-tooling that builds the engines themselves, not the site.
