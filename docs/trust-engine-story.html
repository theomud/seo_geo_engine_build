Chapter 1 · The fear

11 p.m. A question she's scared to ask

Sarah's Dubai contract is ending. In three months she's moving home to London — with Biscuit, her dog. Tonight she reads a post in a pet group and her stomach drops:

"without it your dog will be taken away in airport and never give back from you… still when I remember I crying."

Hands shaking, she types into Google: "what happens if your dog is taken at Dubai airport."
