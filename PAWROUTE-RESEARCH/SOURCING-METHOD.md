# How we sourced this approach (and why we're honest about the confidence)

*This explains where our writing/SEO/GEO method came from, how we derived it, what recurred across
every independent source, and — plainly — what we're sure of versus what is still a hypothesis.*

> **One-line honesty:** this is the **convergent best practice** across all of our research, made
> accurate with official sources. Convergence is strong evidence — but it is **not proof of results.**
> We are confident in the *craft and the facts*; we are **not yet certain it will rank or get cited**,
> because that needs live measurement we haven't run. This is what we *think* will work.

## The method (how we arrived at it)
1. **Gather the corpus.** We pulled from multiple independent expert sources rather than one opinion:
   - *Market Intelligence & SEO Mastery* (30-day system)
   - *Content Intelligence Engine* (lead-gen blog framework)
   - the *Web Persuasion Knowledge Base* + validated `claim_bank.csv` (peer-reviewed/first-party, graded)
   - the *15 Principles* (logic & voice), the *Human Writing System*, the *Regulatory Principles*
   - our own **598-keyword + page-brief research** and **Voice-of-Customer mining** (forums/reviews)
2. **Find what recurs.** We kept the ideas that showed up **independently, again and again**, across
   sources that didn't cite each other. Repetition across independent experts = signal, not opinion.
3. **Keep only what's verifiable.** Every *fact* must trace to an **official source** (government,
   standards body, or the operator's own site) and is screenshotted into the regulatory register.
   Anything we couldn't verify is **hedged or flagged**, never asserted.
4. **Encode it.** The recurring, verified patterns became the **writer prompt** (how we draft) and the
   **auditor** (how we score) — so the method is applied consistently, not by memory.
5. **Produce → verify → (measure).** Write → screenshot-verify claims → human approve. Measurement of
   *results* (rankings/citations) is the last step, and it's **not done yet**.

## What came up time after time (the patterns that drove every decision)
Each of these recurred across **most or all** of the sources above — that's why we build this way:
1. **Answer the fear behind the search, not the keyword.** (SEO Mastery · KB · 15 Principles · VoC)
2. **Front-load the answer** (inverted pyramid / answer-first). (NN/G · GEO study · SEO Mastery · KB)
3. **Official sources only; never guess.** (Regulatory Principles · SEO Mastery · our truth policy)
4. **Original insight + a story beat generic AI text.** (Human Writing System · Content Intelligence · Godin/Housel)
5. **Transparent pricing — cost is the #1 unspoken fear.** (VoC mining · niche audit · our keyword data)
6. **One page, one job; single help-first CTA; funnel-aware.** (Content Intelligence · CXL/GoodUI · KB)
7. **Trust beats volume — 10 great pages > 500 thin ones.** (every source)
8. **GEO ≠ SEO: be quotable, cited, structured to be *retrieved* by AI.** (GEO study · KB · Content Intelligence)

## What we are 100% sure of
Only two categories qualify as **certain** — and we define "certain" strictly:
1. **A fact we have screenshot-verified against its official source, as of the verify date.**
   *Example: "UK requires the rabies vaccination ≥21 full days before travel" — GOV.UK, screenshot
   9 Jun 2026.* We are 100% sure that's what the official source said on that date. (Caveat by design:
   regulations change — hence every claim carries a **re-verify-by** date in the register. Certainty is
   "true as verified on [date]", not "true forever".)
2. **That we applied the method** — answer-first, fear-led, official-sourced, single CTA, etc. We can
   prove we *did* the documented best practice; that's a process certainty.

Everything else is **not** 100%. In particular, any fact we could **not** screenshot from an official
source (e.g. some UAE/MOCCAE import specifics, restricted-breed lists, origin-country export details)
is **stated as "confirm with [authority]"**, not as certainty — and is listed per blog under
`sourcing.stated_but_unverified`.

## What we are NOT sure of (and how we'll find out)
- That it will **rank** (depends on off-page authority/links/age + indexing — not yet built/measured).
- That it will be **AI-cited** (plausible, our strongest shot, but volatile and unproven).
- **How we'll know:** deploy + index → Google Search Console (position/CTR) → `audit/ai_citation.py`
  baseline + monthly → real Core Web Vitals. Until then, scores are *advisory proxies*, not outcomes.

## How this shows up in each blog
Every blog's JSON carries a **`sourcing`** block: the official source behind each fact, the recurring
principle behind each structural choice, and an explicit **confidence** note. So anyone can see *why*
the page is written the way it is — and exactly where our certainty ends. See `METHODOLOGY.md` for the
full writing/audit/imagery manual.
