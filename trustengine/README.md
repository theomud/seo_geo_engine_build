## Why it matters for writing
- **Source bank + 143 screenshots** = the proof behind every cited fact (MOCCAE/airlines/etc.) — feeds the *cite-or-hedge* rule and the "show sourced proof" move.
- **Fear DB + customer profile** = the real fears + exact owner words → fear-led hooks and VoC copy.
- **Competitor evidence** = ammunition for the "alternative"/comparison pages (the top build-first briefs).
- **Content-structure 5-layer + worked examples** = the page skeleton.
