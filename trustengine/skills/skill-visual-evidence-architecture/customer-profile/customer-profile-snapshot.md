## Why this matters for visual evidence

A visual is judged on one thing: **does it lower the specific fear this reader arrived
with?** Not whether it looks nice. So the builder needs to know the reader's fear per
page, which claims they won't believe without seeing proof, and which data must be
drawn as a phone-rendered infographic. This snapshot is exactly that — nothing else
from the master profile is needed to build the briefs.

---

## The reader → why proof must be visible

The customer is **not buying transport — they are buying certainty.** They arrive
afraid and **distrustful**: they have read ten sites that all *say* "trusted" and one
quoted them an outrageous price anyway. Words no longer move them; visible proof does.
*"Every website says something different — I don't know who to trust."*

| Persona | The fear the page's visual must lower | Page |
|---------|--------------------------------------|------|
| Import-to-Dubai owner | Confiscation — "will I actually get my dog back?" | dog taken at the airport |
| Last-Minute Mover | "Can I even fly this month?" | summer embargo |
| Cost-anxious owner | "Am I being overcharged?" | titer cost |
| Confused Researcher | "Which airport/airline is least risky?" | airport comparison |

## The claims that need proof shown → screenshots

Maximum-risk niche: the reader won't believe these without seeing the official source.
Each becomes a **dated official-source screenshot**, tied to its C-ID.

| Claim | Source to screenshot | C-ID |
|-------|----------------------|------|
| Rabies titer required for entry | MOCCAE export/import rule page | C-019 |
| MOCCAE release fee **500 AED/dog** | MOCCAE fee page | C-003 |
| Import permit valid **90 days** | MOCCAE permit page | C-010 |
| Etihad pet fee **USD 399** (official) vs **USD 1,500** (community) | Etihad fee page + community screenshot | C-015 |
| Titer cost — **no official price published** | the official page showing no figure (the honest hedge, made visible) | C-001 |

Official URL anchor: the MOCCAE export-services page (moccae.gov.ae) is the primary
screenshot source.

## The data that becomes infographics → phone-first

| Infographic | Data it visualises | Sourcing |
|-------------|--------------------|----------|
| Summer-embargo calendar | the embargo windows per airline | each date C-ID or HEDGE; verify per airline |
| Airport-comparison table | Sharjah vs Dubai vs Abu Dhabi — cabin rules, fees, friction | C-015 for the Etihad fee; routing detail hedged as COMMUNITY |

Both must render at **390px** — most of this audience is on a phone, mid-panic,
comparing options.

---

## What "real photo" means here → the operator's actual process

**The brand promise:** *"Your dog is not cargo. Your dog is family. We manage the
process so you know what needs to happen, when, and how your dog gets there safely."*
A real photo proves that promise: the actual crate being prepared, the vet's titer
check, the airport handover — this operator, this dog. **Not stock.** A stock golden
retriever proves nothing and is ignored (P-39). The real handover photo is the
un-fakeable proof a competitor's stock page can't match.

---

## How to use this snapshot

Building a page brief: read the persona + fear → judge every visual on whether it
lowers *that* fear; read the claims table → specify the dated screenshot + C-ID for
each; read the data table → build the infographic phone-first; read the brand promise
→ specify the real process photo. Everything else from the master profile is
unnecessary for the visual brief.
