Summer Embargo Calendar

when airlines stop carrying pets — plan around it, not into it
Why there's an embargo. It isn't a sales tactic. Airlines restrict or refuse live animals in cargo when ground temperatures are too high to be safe — commonly around 29.4°C (85°F) at any point in the journey. Dubai's summer routinely hits 40°C+, so pet cargo is widely restricted. The calendar below maps that risk to Dubai's actual monthly temperatures.
