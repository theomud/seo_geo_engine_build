# Screenshot Integration Guide
## How an official-source screenshot becomes visible proof beside a claim
## Niche: Dubai pet relocation · Built 2026-05-30

---

## The principle

A screenshot is proof only if the reader can (a) see what it proves, (b) trust it is
real and current, and (c) find it **right next to the claim** — not in a gallery or
behind a link. This guide is the capture-and-placement standard every official-source
screenshot in the four gap-page briefs must meet. *(Library: P-06 Screenshots Are
Proof; F-08 Proof Interstitial; M-13 Proof Density.)*

---

## The capture standard (every screenshot)

| Element | Rule | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| **Legible claim** | Frame so the fee/rule/figure is readable at the size it will appear on the page | An unreadable screenshot proves nothing |
| **Date stamp** | Capture with the date visible, or annotate "captured YYYY-MM-DD" | The date is the proof — it shows the rule existed on that day (P-06) |
| **Source URL** | Record the exact official page URL; show it in the caption | Lets the reader verify; proves it's the regulator's own page |
| **C-ID** | Tie the screenshot to its Source Bank row | Links visible proof back to the verified claim |
| **Caption** | One line: what this is + why it matters | Tells the wary reader what they're looking at |

A screenshot missing the date or the source URL is **rejected** — it is an image, not
proof (File 03, gates A2/A3).

---

## The placement standard (proof interstitial)

Place the screenshot **immediately beside the claim it supports**, so it interrupts
doubt at the moment it arises:

```
  [ claim sentence in the copy ]
  ┌───────────────────────────────┐
  │  [ official-source screenshot ]│
  │  caption: what + why · C-ID    │
  │  source: <official URL> · date │
  └───────────────────────────────┘
  [ copy continues ]
```

Never: a row of screenshots at the bottom of the page (a gallery proves nothing about
any one claim), or a "view source" link the reader won't click.

## The honest-absence capture (the hardest case)

When the proof is that **no official figure exists** (the titer price), screenshot the
official page *showing the absence* — the MOCCAE/lab page with no fixed titer fee —
and caption it honestly. Showing that you looked and found nothing official converts
the cost-anxious reader better than a confident fake number. This is the skill's
signature move: the hedge, made visible.

---

## Checklist before a screenshot ships

- [ ] Claim legible at final display size
- [ ] Date visible or annotated
- [ ] Exact official source URL recorded and in the caption
- [ ] C-ID linked to the Source Bank row
- [ ] One-line caption (what + why)
- [ ] Placed beside the claim, not in a gallery
- [ ] Captured from the live official source, not a cache or third-party copy

Any unchecked box → recapture before publishing.
