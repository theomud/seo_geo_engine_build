## PHASE 1 — MARKET RESEARCH ✅ DONE

**What was researched:** why a frightened, distrustful reader doesn't believe words; how stock photography fails (ignored as ad-like); how visible, dated proof builds trust at the decision moment.
**Key findings:** in a high-fear market, **trust is built with the eyes** — a claim the reader can't *see proven* is worth nothing. Visuals must be **evidence with a job** (lower a named fear by showing), never decoration. Real photos beat stock measurably (+102.5%, 37signals).
**Sources / library:** M-37 Real-Photo Conversion-Lift, M-13 Proof Density, M-19 Nielsen's 4 Credibility Factors; F-08 Proof Interstitial, F-07 Documentation Loop, F-04 Source Verification; P-06 Screenshots Are Proof, P-39 Replace Stock, P-40 Contextual Relevance.
**Verdict:** high-commercial, highly teachable; visible-proof-beside-the-claim is the un-fakeable asset competitors' stock pages can't match.

## WHAT THIS SKILL PROVED

**Core finding:** visible, dated, official-source proof beside the claim is what converts a distrustful reader — and the discipline is testable (≥1 proof-visible screenshot per page; every infographic render-checked at 390px). The hardest case proved the rule: when no official figure exists, you **screenshot the absence** rather than invent a number.
**What changed from the spec:** the summer-embargo infographic moved from "exact per-airline dates" to "real climate normals + hedged airline specifics" once the dates couldn't be verified — honesty over false precision.
**What others don't do:** 9/9 competitors use stock imagery and assert trust in words; none show the regulator's own dated page beside the claim.
