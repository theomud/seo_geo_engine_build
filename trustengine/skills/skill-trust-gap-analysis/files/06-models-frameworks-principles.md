## MODELS — the data structures and mental models this skill uses

- **M-05 ⟳ — Trust Score Model.** The 10-dimension per-page trust score. *Update:* cross-referenced to NN/g's 5-level Hierarchy of Trust and E-E-A-T's four pillars. *Source:* NN/g; Google QRG.
- **M-24 ★ — E-E-A-T Four-Pillar Trust Model** (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — Trust at the centre). The lens every competitor page is read through. *Source:* Google QRG, rev. 11 Sept 2025.
- **M-18 — NN/g Hierarchy of Trust** (5 commitment levels). The published canon the 10-point score maps onto. *Source:* Aurora Harley, Nielsen Norman Group.
- *(skill-internal: the **Risk Continuum** — 4 risk levels setting the niche's proof threshold — and the **Content Gap Matrix** remain this skill's signature constructs; candidates for future library codes.)*

## FRAMEWORKS — the decision systems and processes

- **F-03 ⟳ — Trust Score Competitor Scoring.** Score every competitor page on the 10 dimensions. *Update:* map the 10 onto NN/g's 53-recommendation report (Vol. 09) to defend the cut. *Source:* NN/g.
- **F-11 — Forty-Five-Check Audit.** The deeper page-quality audit behind the 10-point score. *Source:* maps onto NN/g's 53 trust recommendations.
- **F-31 — "Who / How / Why" Self-Assessment Loop.** Applied to competitors: who stands behind each claim, how it is proven, why a reader should trust it. *Source:* Google Search Central.

## PRINCIPLES — the non-negotiable rules

- **P-22 — Trust Is the Centre; the Other Three Pillars Collapse Without It.** A competitor strong on expertise but weak on trust still loses. *Source:* Google QRG §3.4 — "Trust is the most important member of the E-E-A-T family."
- **P-03 — Proof Over Promise.** When two competitors give identical advice, undeniable real-world proof wins; most score 3–5/10 for lack of proof, not information. *Source:* Fogg et al. (2003).
- **P-02 — Wrong Information Causes Real Harm.** In a maximum-risk niche every claim must trace to an official source; generic reassurance fails. *Source:* YMYL (Google QRG).
