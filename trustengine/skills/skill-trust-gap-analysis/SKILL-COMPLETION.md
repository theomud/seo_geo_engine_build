## PHASE 1 — MARKET RESEARCH

**What was researched:**
- [x] Google search for existing competitor-analysis frameworks and tools
- [x] Published trust/credibility research (NN/g, Google QRG, Hormozi)
- [x] Competitor tools/services that do competitor scoring
- [x] Current standard practice for competitor content audits

**Key findings:**
Standard competitor research catalogues what competitors *publish* (keywords, backlinks, page counts). It does not score what they *fail to say* — the unacknowledged fears and uncited claims that lose the sale in a high-stakes market. The gap this skill fills: a repeatable, risk-calibrated **trust** score plus a frequency-ranked gap matrix that converts the audit directly into a build order. Novel combination: the Risk Continuum (sets the proof bar) + a 10-point Trust Score + the Content Gap Matrix.

**Sources reviewed:**
| Source | URL | Key Insight |
|--------|-----|-------------|
| NN/g — Hierarchy of Trust (Aurora Harley) | nngroup.com | 5 experiential levels of website commitment; corroborates the 10-point score |
| Google Search Quality Rater Guidelines (rev. 11 Sep 2025) | google.com | E-E-A-T; "Trust is the most important member of the E-E-A-T family" (§3.4); YMYL |
| Hormozi — "The New Way of Making Content in the Age of AI" | — | Risk Continuum (4 levels); proof must be undeniable; proof interstitial throughout |
| NN/g — Trust & Credibility: Ecommerce UX (Vol. 09) | nngroup.com | 53 trust recommendations; the published canon the 45-check audit maps onto |

**Market research verdict:**
Improved + novel. Trust auditing exists in fragments (NN/g, E-E-A-T) but no published framework combines a risk-calibrated proof threshold, a 10-dimension trust score, and a failure-frequency gap matrix that outputs a content build order. (Full external citations: MFP-LIBRARY.md — M-04, M-05, M-18, M-24, F-03.)

## PHASE 4 — AUTOMATION

**What was automated:**
Scoring the long tail of competitors: Playwright visits each URL, screenshots homepage/service/about, extracts text; the Anthropic API scores all 10 dimensions with a one-line evidence note each and writes JSON + TXT score files.

**Engine built:** yes — skill-trust-gap-analysis/engines/competitor_research_engine.py (spec: engines/engine-trust-gap-analysis.md + files/04-automation-spec.md)
**Automation level:** ~70% (humans keep risk placement, discovery, top-3 calibration, low-confidence review)
**Cost per run:** ≈ $0.05–0.15 per competitor (Anthropic, claude-sonnet-4-6); full 9-competitor run < $1.50
**Time per run:** ~2–4 minutes for the auto-scored set (2s page pause + 0.5s API pause per competitor)

**Automation test results:**
The engine auto-scored **6 of 9** competitors; the **3** scored manually first served as the calibration set. Auto and manual scores agreed within the audit threshold. Dimensions 2 (official source cited) and 10 (proof interstitial) are the two most prone to false positives and are routed to human review when ambiguous.

## WHAT THIS SKILL PROVED

**The core finding:**
In a maximum-risk niche, competitors do not lose on information — they lose on **proof**. The market average is 3.9/10 not because providers lack knowledge but because they assert it without citation, bury their credentials, and never name the customer's deepest fear. The first provider to score the gaps and build them wins.

**What changed from the original spec:**
Point 10 was redefined from "has testimonials" to "proof interstitial throughout" (proof beside each claim, not one block) following Hormozi's analysis. Community discovery was elevated to a required step after 4/9 competitors proved undiscoverable via Google alone.

**What competitors or published frameworks do not do:**
No competitor (and no off-the-shelf SEO tool) sets a risk-calibrated proof threshold before scoring, nor ranks gaps by cross-competitor failure frequency to produce a build order. The closest published anchors (NN/g Hierarchy of Trust, E-E-A-T) describe trust but do not operationalise a competitive gap audit.

## HOW TO APPLY TO A NEW NICHE

**What changes per niche:**
- The competitor list (discovered via that market's Google + communities)
- The niche's Risk Continuum level (sets the proof bar)
- The fears that the dimensions resolve (from that market's customer research)
- The universal gaps that emerge from that market's matrix

**What stays the same:**
- The Risk Continuum, the 10-Point Trust Score, the Content Gap Matrix
- The 4-step process and the manual-then-automated calibration
- The engine (point it at a new competitor list) and the 45-check audit

**Time estimate for a new niche:**
Manual: ~half a day (discovery + top-3 scoring + matrix)
Automated: ~2–4 minutes for the auto-scored set
