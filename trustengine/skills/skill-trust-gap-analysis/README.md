## STEP 0 — The Risk Continuum (do this before scoring anyone)

Before scoring a single competitor, place the niche on the Risk Continuum.
This determines how much proof every page in the market needs.

### The 4 Risk Levels

**Low Risk — Entertainment**
User risks seconds of their time. If it fails, nothing is lost.
Examples: memes, comedy, light lifestyle content.
Proof threshold: Low. Personality and entertainment value win.

**Medium-Low Risk — B2C Education**
User follows advice that affects appearance or minor habits.
Examples: hair tutorials, fitness tips, basic cooking.
Proof threshold: Moderate. Live demonstration beats text advice.

**Medium-High Risk — B2C Financial / Legal**
Real money or legal standing is involved.
Examples: investing advice, legal guidance, insurance decisions.
Proof threshold: High. Third-party credentials required.
Biggest creators here have decades of track record or professional
qualifications that cannot be faked.

**High Risk — Regulated Services**
Wrong advice causes irreversible harm.
Examples: medical treatment, immigration, pet relocation, property law.
Proof threshold: Maximum. Every claim must trace to an official source.
A pet confiscated at the border because of wrong advice is a
catastrophic failure. This niche is maximum risk.

### Why This Matters For Scoring

If two competitors give identical advice, the one with undeniable
real-world proof always wins. In maximum-risk niches, most competitors
score 3-5/10 not because they lack information but because they provide
zero proof that their information is correct.

**Dubai pet relocation sits at maximum risk on the continuum.**
This is why 9 of 9 competitors failed the airport confiscation question —
the question demands official source proof, not generic reassurance.

## The 10-Point Trust Score

For every competitor page, one point for each:

| # | Question | Yes | No |
|---|----------|-----|----|
| 1 | Fear in first 100 words? | Opens with customer's specific worry | "Professional Pet Services" |
| 2 | Official source cited? | Links to government or regulatory body | Claims without citations |
| 3 | Specific route/variant named? | Not generic — names the specific case | "All international routes" |
| 4 | Step-by-step process? | Numbered steps with timing | Generic bullet points |
| 5 | Timeline included? | Specific durations, not vague | No time references |
| 6 | Cost ranges shown? | Real numbers with honest disclaimers | "Contact us for a quote" |
| 7 | Common mistakes section? | Edge cases addressed | No edge cases |
| 8 | Original visuals? | Real photos not stock | Stock photos only |
| 9 | CTA feels like help? | Assists not pressures | Hard sales push |
| 10 | Proof interstitial? | Proof embedded throughout (not just one section) | Proof only in testimonials |

**Note on Point 10:** This criterion was updated following Hormozi's analysis.
Proof must be embedded throughout the content — beside each step, each claim,
each process instruction. One testimonials section does not count.

**Score bands:**
0-2: Displace immediately
3-4: Weak — exploit gaps
5-6: Decent — specific gaps to target
7-8: Strong — study and surpass
9-10: Excellent — learn from everything

## The 4 Steps Inside This Skill

**Step 00 — Risk Continuum Placement**
Where does this niche sit? What proof threshold does that set?
Do this before visiting a single competitor website.

**Step 01 — Competitor Discovery**
Find every competitor. Not just Google page 1. Community research
(Facebook groups, Reddit) surfaces competitors Google tools miss.
In the proof niche, 4 of 9 scored competitors were only discoverable
through community research.

**Step 02 — Manual Scoring (top 3)**
Score the top 3 manually first. Calibrates judgment before automation.
Screenshot every page. Score every dimension. Document every gap.

**Step 03 — Automated Scoring (remaining)**
Playwright visits every remaining competitor. Extracts page text.
Anthropic API scores against all 10 dimensions.

**Step 04 — Gap Matrix**
Count how many competitors fail each dimension.
Most failures = highest-priority content to build first.
