What This Skill Is

Most competitor research tells you what competitors publish. This skill tells you what they fail to say — and why that gap is worth money.

The core insight: In high-stakes markets, customers choose the provider they trust most — not the cheapest, not the one with the most pages. Trust is built by acknowledging fears and providing verified proof. The gaps where competitors fail to do this are your highest-value content opportunities.

In the Dubai pet relocation proof run, 9 of 9 scored competitors failed to address the deepest fear in the market — a dog confiscated at the airport and never returned. That is not a coincidence. Most content teams write what sounds safe and professional. They do not write what customers are actually afraid of.

Before scoring a single competitor, place the niche on the Risk Continuum. This determines the proof threshold for every page in the market.

Dubai pet relocation = maximum risk. Wrong advice means a pet confiscated at the border. This means every page in this market must meet maximum proof standards. Most competitors score 3-5/10 because they provide zero proof their information is correct.

The risk level also determines what gaps matter most. In a low-risk market, missing a comparison page is a missed opportunity. In a maximum-risk market, missing the airport confiscation fear page is a failure to serve the customer's most important need.

Point 10 — Proof Interstitial

This scoring dimension was updated after Hormozi's analysis. Most competitors have testimonials somewhere. Almost none have proof embedded throughout their content.

The distinction: Testimonial section at the bottom = weak. Official source screenshot beside the step that requires it = strong.

What passes Point 10

  • Screenshot of MOCCAE export page beside the export step
  • Airline policy screenshot beside the breed restriction warning
  • Real case date + route beside the timeline claim
  • Verbatim official quote beside the regulation claim
  • Documented failure beside the common mistakes section

What fails Point 10

  • Generic testimonial: "Great service! Highly recommend!"
  • Stock photo of a dog at an airport
  • Testimonials section at page bottom only
  • Third-party claims: "As featured in..." without link
  • Statistics without sources

The 4 Steps

Step 00 — Risk Continuum Placement

Where does this niche sit? What proof threshold does that set? Do this before visiting a single competitor. Dubai pet relocation = maximum risk.

Step 01 — Competitor Discovery

Find every competitor using: Google autocomplete, Google page 1, Facebook groups, Reddit threads. Not just Google tools. In the Dubai proof run, 4 of 9 scored competitors were only discoverable through community research.

Step 02 — Manual Scoring (top 3)

Score the top 3 by hand first. This calibrates judgment before the automation runs. Screenshot every page. Score every dimension. Document every gap.

Step 03 — Automated Scoring

Playwright visits every remaining competitor. Extracts text. Anthropic API scores against all 10 dimensions. Saves JSON + screenshots automatically.

Step 04 — Gap Matrix

Count failures per dimension across all competitors. Sort by most failures. The top gaps are your content calendar for the next 6 months.
