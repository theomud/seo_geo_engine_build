OUR STORY
OUR JOURNEY SO FAR

Our founders were faced with an exceptionally challenging dilemma a few years ago, one faced by nearly every person who has ever had a pet. They required a service where their beloved pets could travel alongside them, in the same comfort and grandeur that they did, safely. Upon searching for such a service, they found that there was no provider which met their needs for a comfortable and convenient trip with their pet. So, they decided to start such a service, and in 2019, Carry My Pet was born. Today, Carry My Pet is powered by a passionate team of 250+ skilled professionals, working together to ensure every journey is stress-free, safe, and personalised.

WHAT WE DO
OUR PHILOSOPHY

A simple vision led the two brothers, Faisal & Aamir Islam, to co-founding the company; the goal of providing a safe, comfortable, reliable, and premium pet transportation service to everybody across the country. Jump a few years ahead in time, with over 40+ international locations covered, over 12,000 successful journeys, and over 70 cities mapped thoroughly via multiple means of transport, it is safe to say their vision has come to fruition.
