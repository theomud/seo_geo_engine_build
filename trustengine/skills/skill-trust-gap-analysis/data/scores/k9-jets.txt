About Us
The World’s First Pet-Dedicated, Pay-per-Seat, Private Jet Service

K9 JETS launched in March 2023 to revolutionise the way pets travel.

Since then, we’ve proudly flown over 7000 pets and their owners across the Atlantic, Europe, and the Middle East, offering a stress-free, crate-free, cabin-side experience like no other.

As the The original British-based, ATOL-protected, pay-by-seat private jet service for pets and their owners, we’re dedicated to delivering a truly first-class experience — always by your side.
