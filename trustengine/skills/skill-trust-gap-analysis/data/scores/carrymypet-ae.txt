GET TO KNOW US

Carry My Pet is a premium, global pet relocation service designed for discerning pet parents who expect precision, discretion, and absolute care.

With 12,000+ successful relocations across 40+ countries, CarryMyPet.ae operates as a dedicated international hub from Dubai, managing complex pet movements with seamless coordination and meticulous attention to detail.

From regulatory approvals and airline handling to customs clearance and final delivery, every journey is curated end-to-end by specialists who understand that your pet’s comfort and safety are non-negotiable. No rushed processes. No uncertainty. Just refined, reliable pet transportation handled with the same care you would expect for family.

OUR STORY
OUR JOURNEY THIS FAR

We were faced with a challenging dilemma a few years ago, one faced by nearly every person who has ever had a pet. We required a service where our beloved pets could travel alongside us, in the same comfort and grandeur that we did. Upon searching for such a service, we didn’t quite find anything catering to our specific needs. So, we decided to start it ourselves, and in 2019, Carry My Pet was born.

The objective of this service was to provide a safe, comfortable, reliable, and premium pet transportation service accessible to anyone around the world. Jumping a few years ahead in time, with over 40 international locations covered, over 12,000 successful journeys, it is safe to say our vision has come to fruition.

WHAT WE DO
OUR PHILOSOPHY

What began as a simple idea between two brothers, Faisal and Aamir Islam, soon transformed into a mission: to make pet transportation safe, reliable, and luxurious for everyone. Today, with operations spanning 40+ countries and 13,000 journeys completed, their vision has truly taken flight.

OUR VISION
OUR MISSION
OUR VALUES

Carry My Pet was founded to provide a safe and reliable journey to every pet parent globally. Having started operations within one hub, we now aim to have a presence globally & to serve as the go-to operator for simplifying international pet travel.
