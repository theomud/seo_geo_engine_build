Global Mobility and Relocation
What is Global Mobility?

Global mobility is an increasing trend in an increasing mobile, modern global workforce. It refers to the relocation of employees for work purposes.

With strong economic growth in the Middle East and Asia progressively outstripping that of Europe, companies are moving away from the “business trip” approach to globalisation in favour of permanent posts in lands far from home.

In turn, this is increasing the prevalence of global mobility, meaning that HR teams face an ever growing burden from processing the required documentation.

If this resonates with you, we can help.

What are the benefits of Employee Mobility?

Employee mobility in a global environment comes with some great benefits; whether you’re looking to save money, increase cultural immersion or simply explore new opportunities, a global mobility program will benefit you.

Long Term Cost Savings

Although initially it may seem like the process of setting up an in-house global mobility program is costly, long term, it will save money. While managing it yourself can add to your HR burden, it will pay off.

With a globally mobile business, you can unlock the potential of different rates of corporation tax, cheaper manufacturing costs and other local benefits that would not otherwise be a consideration to your business.

Access the Local Talent Pool

The effects of global talent shortages are a real concern in modern business and it’s clear that these can be mitigated by expanding into or relocating to another region.

Once a global mobility process is in place, it becomes immediately possible to tap into a wider talent pool, potentially leading to greator problem solving abilities within your team.

Diversity, Creativity and Insights

Further to problem solving, differences in culture can introduce insight into a business that may have previously not been present. From this stems creativity and diversity, that can really help a business to flourish internationally.
