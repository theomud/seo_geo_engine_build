## MODELS — the data structures and mental models this skill uses

- **M-01 — Verified Fact Model.** The re-verification rule operates on this: every Source Bank fact has a perishable verified-date. *Source:* Google QRG factual-accuracy requirement.
- **M-06 — Source Bank Model.** The store of facts a page is refreshed against when an entry expires. *Source:* internal.
- **M-11 — Content Depth Model.** Decides whether a thin page is expanded or pruned. *Source:* "comprehensive content" pillar (Nielsen 1999).

## FRAMEWORKS — the decision systems and processes

- **F-12 — Content Update Priority Matrix.** `situation → action → priority` queue (high-traffic-declining = first; high-traffic-strong = monitor only). *Source:* internal.
- **F-35 — Quarterly Refresh Cadence.** The default refresh rhythm for the whole content estate. *Source:* Semrush 2025 — "quarterly refreshes yield 42% better results than annual."
- **F-25 — GSC + GA4 Dual-Source Pattern.** Step-3 "optimise for search" reads CTR/ranking from GSC and behaviour from GA4. *Source:* developers.google.com search-console docs.

## PRINCIPLES — the non-negotiable rules

- **P-41 — Update 20–30% of Textual Content (not just dates).** A date bump without substantive change does not earn a refresh. *Source:* Google guidance; Quattr 2026 freshness data.
- **P-42 — Refresh, Consolidate, Prune, or Redirect Decision Rule.** Every page resolves to one fate; cannibalising pages are merged with 301s. *Source:* Animalz / Fractl content-decay playbooks.
- **P-08 ⟳ — Re-verify Every 90 Days.** Expired Source Bank facts are re-checked at the official URL before the page is republished. *Source:* Semrush 2025; Ahrefs July 2025.
