Skill 02 — Source Bank

Every regulation claim verified against a named official source. Nothing publishes without a Source Bank row.

5 mandatory fields

Claim · the exact statement that will appear on a page
Official URL · government / airline / industry-body page
Date checked · must be <90 days for Verified
Exact quote · verbatim — no paraphrase, no ellipsis
Plain English · one customer-readable sentence, same meaning

Status outcomes

Verified
All 5 gates passed. Writers use Plain English + link URL.
Conflicting
Community says X, official says Y. Lead with official, note discrepancy on the page.
Unverifiable
No official source publishes the claim. Hedge: "No official figure exists; community range is …"
Pending —
load failed / Manual / parse failed / Manual review required. Human must verify in a real browser.

Source authority — stop at the first that publishes

  1. Regulating government body (e.g. MOCCAE for UAE)
  2. Destination country's animal-health authority (APHA · DAFF · CFIA · DALRRD · USDA APHIS · DAHD)
  3. Airline operating the route (Emirates · Etihad · Turkish · Royal Jordanian · Air Cairo)
  4. Recognised industry body (IPATA) — only when no government source exists
  5. Otherwise → mark Unverifiable. Never substitute a blog, agency, or forum post.

The 5 gates of Verified

  1. Authority — official-domain URL, not a competitor or blog
  2. Verbatim quote — copy/paste, original punctuation
  3. Plain-English fidelity — same meaning; don't soften "must" to "may"
  4. Date — <90 days
  5. Screenshot — full page → [country]-[authority]-[claim-id]-[date].png

Maintenance triggers

date > 90 days → re-verify
regulator rule change → re-verify category in 7 days
audit pass < 90% → halt content + re-run manual
new niche → run the whole skill from scratch

Audit standard

20% sample · 4 gates · pass at ≥ 90%

The audit reads only Claim + URL — re-fetches the page and re-extracts the quote itself. Never reads the existing Quote or Plain English fields.

Real findings — Dubai pet relocation

✓ 5 of 5 UK facets Verified via gov.uk — quarantine, breed restrictions, timeline (21-day rabies wait), documents, requirements.
⚠ MOCCAE prices Unverifiable — titer 700–1,300 AED, vet release 500 AED, fit-to-fly 200 AED. No regulator-published figures. "No official source publishes this cost. Community-sourced price range: [amount]."
⚠ AU / IN / ZA blocked headless — gov sites either WAF-block or DNS-fail. Manual verification needed.
