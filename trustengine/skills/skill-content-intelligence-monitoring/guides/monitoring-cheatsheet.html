The RICE formula

RICE = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort. R/I/C push up, Effort pushes down — a cheap, certain, high-consequence fix beats an expensive, speculative one. R 1–10 · I 0.25/0.5/1/2/3 · C 0.5–1.0 · E 1–5
FactorMeansTop of scale
Reachcustomers/pages hit10 = every customer
Impactworst case if ignored3 = real harm (pet denied)
Confidencehow sure it's real1.0 = official source
Effortdays to act5 = new full asset
