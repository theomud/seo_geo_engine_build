Content Intelligence Monitoring

Watch everything that can change the truth, score every signal by consequence, and act on the few that matter. A page that's quietly wrong is the most dangerous state there is — because someone trusts it.

Why this skill

Every page is true the day you publish it and decaying from the next. The regulation gets amended, the airline rewrites its cargo rule, the fee goes up, a competitor fills the gap, a community thread surfaces a fear nobody answered. None of it announces itself — the page keeps looking authoritative while it slowly stops being correct.

"Every website says something different — I don't know who to trust." — the customer who is now trusting a page that was right last quarter and is wrong this one.

Monitoring nothing means you learn a regulation changed when a pet is already stuck. Monitoring everything and reacting to all of it means you drown, and the quiet regulatory change is lost under the loud competitor noise. This skill does both right: watch broadly across 12 streams, score every signal by consequence, act on the few that matter.

Score by consequence not volume

A monitor that surfaces signals without ranking them just relocates the overwhelm. The breakthrough is one scoring rule applied to everything, so the queue is sorted by consequence — and in this niche the most important signal is often the quietest. M-27 F-05

Volume wins (the trap)
Act on the loudest alert → a competitor's price grumble jumps the queue → the quiet MOCCAE change is missed → a pet is denied entry.
Consequence wins
RICE scores both: re-verification clock 24.3 → today; price grumble 9.6 → this week. The number decides, not the volume.

The RICE formula

Every signal — regulatory alert or community grumble — passes through the same rule. M-27

RICE = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort
FactorScaleWhat it means here
Reach1–10customers / pages affected (10 = every import customer)
Impact0.25–33 = massive — wrong info → a pet denied entry (the YMYL bar)
Confidence0.5–1.01.0 = official source; ~0.8 = community pattern; 0.5 = single report
Effort1–5days to act (1 = same-day edit; 5 = new asset)
Why the shape matters: Reach, Impact and Confidence push a signal up; Effort pushes it down. A cheap, certain, high-consequence fix outranks an expensive, speculative one — automatically. P-02 Impact 3 is reserved for real harm; P-07 Confidence is capped by the source — community-only can't exceed ~0.8, however loud.

Keeping it honest

A monitoring system fails in ways that look like success. Four defences catch them:

  • Engine-scored — re-compute every RICE from its R/I/C/E; a score that doesn't reproduce was typed, not computed.
  • Routed by band — re-derive every band from the score; a band that doesn't match route() was chosen, not routed.
  • Sourced — re-trace every signal to a C-ID, the gap matrix, a named quote, or the profile; a sourceless signal is a rumour in the queue.
  • Honest coverage — a live reading from an unconnected stream is a fabrication; no key means awaiting connection, and the report says so.

A typed score, a chosen band, a sourceless signal, a fabricated reading, or a report that drifts between runs is a hard fail. And the live monitor only counts once the keys are set and a real detected change flows through the same RICE engine — flagged NOT YET CONFIRMED until then. P-07 P-08
