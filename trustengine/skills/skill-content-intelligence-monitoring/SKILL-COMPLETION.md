## PHASE 1 — MARKET RESEARCH ✅ DONE

**What was researched:** how published content decays (regulations amended, airline policies
rewritten, fees changed, competitors filling gaps, community fears shifting) and how to detect it
before it harms a customer; prioritisation models that turn a firehose of signals into a ranked
action queue.
**Key findings:** monitoring without ranking just relocates the overwhelm — the breakthrough is a
single scoring rule (**RICE = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort**, M-27) applied to every
signal so the queue is sorted by *consequence*, not volume, and the quiet high-stakes signal beats
the loud trivial one. In a YMYL niche (M-31) the Impact scale tops out at real harm (P-02), and the
freshness cadence (P-08, 90-day re-verify) is what keeps the source of truth from silently decaying.
**Sources / library:** M-27, M-31, M-41, M-30; F-05, F-10, F-12, F-39, F-35, F-21; P-02, P-07,
P-08, P-48, P-01.
**Verdict:** the most automatable skill in the program (Automation 5/5) — the engine runs the whole
cycle (collect → score → route → report) end to end; the human keeps only the judgement (calibrating
Impact/Confidence) and the acting.

## WHAT THIS SKILL PROVED

**Core finding:** intelligence is objective and buildable — watch broadly across 12 streams, score
every signal `(R × I × C) ÷ E`, and let `route()` file it by consequence. Engine-scored, fully
sourced, honest about coverage, and reproducible is the bar.
**The counter-intuitive result:** the most important signal is usually the quietest — the regulatory
re-verification clock (24.3, TODAY) outranks the loud price-gouging complaint (9.6, this week). The
engine beats instinct precisely because it scores consequence, not volume.
**What others don't do:** competitors react to whatever is loudest (or nothing); this skill ranks by
harm-weighted consequence and never lets a verified fact silently age past 90 days.

## HOW TO APPLY TO A NEW NICHE

**What changes:** the streams you watch (the regulator, the competitors, the search panel, the
community for *your* market) and the verified sources each signal traces to.
**What stays the same:** watch broadly, score every signal `(R × I × C) ÷ E`, route by band (≥20
TODAY / 15–19.99 48HR / 8–14.99 week / <8 month), re-verify on a 90-day cadence, and never act on
volume. The engine (config + rice_score + route + report) is portable as-is.
**Time:** scoring a signal is seconds once R/I/C/E are set; the engine writes the report in
milliseconds; the cycle runs weekly (with the monthly review for the low-RICE tail).
