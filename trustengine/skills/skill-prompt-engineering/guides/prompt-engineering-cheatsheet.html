The 9 elements

2 Rolewho the AI is
3 Objectivewhat to produce
4 Audiencethe reader's state
5 Inputsfacts (cite by C-ID)

⚑ The two everyone skips — Constraints + Quality Criteria — decide if it's usable.

Minimum Viable Prompt

Context + Role + Objective + Output Format — never fewer."Dubai pet relocation (ctx). Relocation specialist (role). 3 FAQ answers on the summer embargo, hedge what MOCCAE doesn't publish (obj). Markdown Q&A (format)."

The revision loop (2–3 rounds)

Always say what to change AND what to keep."Revise: hedge the 700–1,300 AED line. KEEP: the opening fear sentence + the MOCCAE-silence paragraph."

The 10 mistakes

  1. Vague objective
  2. No audience
  3. Information overload
  4. Conflicting constraints
  5. No quality criteria
  6. One-shot expectation
  7. Not saving good prompts
  8. Ignoring output format
  9. Wrong role
  10. Not comparing models

The 70%-usable rule

Measured, not felt — count edits to publish.
Below 70% → fix the prompt (an element), not the output.
Never assert an Unverifiable claim — hedge it or block it.
