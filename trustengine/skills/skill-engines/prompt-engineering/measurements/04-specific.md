# Specific, not generic

*Measurement 4 of Prompt Engineering (output clarity).*

## What it measures (how)
We measure concrete-figure density.

## Why it works
Specifics are what a good brief forces; generic mush is what a lazy prompt returns.

## What to do (the fix)
Re-prompt with required facts so the output is specific, not generic.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
