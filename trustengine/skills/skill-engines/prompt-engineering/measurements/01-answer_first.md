# Answer-first output

*Measurement 1 of Prompt Engineering (output clarity).*

## What it measures (how)
We check the opening for a direct answer.

## Why it works
Good prompting produces an answer up front; vague prompting produces a warm-up.

## What to do (the fix)
Open with the direct answer (re-prompt for answer-first).

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: A long preamble before the point.
- DO: The direct answer in the first sentence.
