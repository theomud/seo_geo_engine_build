# Right length / focused

*Measurement 5 of Prompt Engineering (output clarity).*

## What it measures (how)
We check word count against a focused range.

## Why it works
Good prompting controls scope; bloat and thinness both signal a weak brief.

## What to do (the fix)
Tighten scope in the prompt — cut padding or thicken thin content.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: 3,000 words of padding.
- DO: ~800 focused words.
