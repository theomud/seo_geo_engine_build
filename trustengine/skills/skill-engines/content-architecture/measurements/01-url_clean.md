# Clean URL

*Measurement 1 of Content Architecture.*

## What it measures (how)
We check the URL for params/uppercase/IDs/extensions.

## Why it works
Clean keyword URLs are readable, shareable, and a deliberate-architecture signal.

## What to do (the fix)
Use a clean lowercase, hyphenated, keyword URL (no params/IDs).

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: /page.php?id=12345&cat=3
- DO: /routes/dubai-to-uk
