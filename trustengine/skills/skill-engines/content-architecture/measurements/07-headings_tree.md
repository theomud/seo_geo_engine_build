# Heading structure

*Measurement 7 of Content Architecture.*

## What it measures (how)
We count H2 sections.

## Why it works
A heading tree is the page-level architecture that makes content scannable and extractable.

## What to do (the fix)
Break the page into clear H2 sections.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: One long block of text.
- DO: H2: Requirements / Timeline / Costs.
