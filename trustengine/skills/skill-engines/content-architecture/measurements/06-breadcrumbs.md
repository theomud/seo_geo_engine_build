# Breadcrumbs

*Measurement 6 of Content Architecture.*

## What it measures (how)
We look for breadcrumb markup.

## Why it works
Breadcrumbs show the page's place in the hierarchy — for users and for search.

## What to do (the fix)
Add breadcrumb navigation (+ BreadcrumbList schema).

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: (no breadcrumbs)
- DO: Home › Routes › Dubai to UK
