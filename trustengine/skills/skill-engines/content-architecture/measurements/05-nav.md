# Semantic navigation

*Measurement 5 of Content Architecture.*

## What it measures (how)
We look for a <nav> element.

## Why it works
A clear nav is the backbone of a navigable architecture.

## What to do (the fix)
Add a semantic <nav> with the main sections.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Links loose in a <div>.
- DO: A semantic <nav> with the main sections.
