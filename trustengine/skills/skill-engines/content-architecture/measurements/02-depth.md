# Shallow click depth

*Measurement 2 of Content Architecture.*

## What it measures (how)
We measure URL path depth.

## Why it works
Pages within ~3 levels of home get crawled and found; deep pages get buried.

## What to do (the fix)
Bring the page within 3 clicks of the homepage.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: /a/b/c/d/e/page (5+ levels)
- DO: /routes/dubai-to-uk (≤3 levels)
