# Official sources linked

*Measurement 1 of Official Source Research.*

## What it measures (how)
We count outbound links to government/authority domains.

## Why it works
A claim with a link to the regulator is provable; an unsourced claim is just an assertion.

## What to do (the fix)
Link the official government/authority page for each regulation/fee.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Dubai requires documents.
- DO: Dubai requires an import permit — see MOCCAE (moccae.gov.ae).
