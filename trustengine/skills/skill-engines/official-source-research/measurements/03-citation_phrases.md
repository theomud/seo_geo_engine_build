# Citation phrasing

*Measurement 3 of Official Source Research.*

## What it measures (how)
We look for 'according to / per the / states that' phrasing.

## Why it works
Explicit attribution makes claims checkable and is what AI engines reward when citing.

## What to do (the fix)
Attribute claims explicitly ('according to MOCCAE…').

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Pets need a titer test.
- DO: According to MOCCAE, pets need a titer test.
