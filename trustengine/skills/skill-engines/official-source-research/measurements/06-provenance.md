# Figure provenance

*Measurement 6 of Official Source Research.*

## What it measures (how)
We compare citation count to the number of figures.

## Why it works
Every number should trace to a source; lots of figures with no citations is a red flag.

## What to do (the fix)
Give every figure a source, or hedge it honestly if none is published.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: A fee figure with no source.
- DO: 500 AED release fee (MOCCAE, 2026).
