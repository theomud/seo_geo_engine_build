# Trust Gap Analysis — Cheatsheet

*The 10 trust signals that decide whether a worried visitor believes you enough to act.*

| # | Measurement | What to do | Example (don't &rarr; do) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fear named in first 100 words | Name the customer's real fear in the first 100 words. | DON'T: Welcome — your trusted pet partner. &rarr; DO: Worried your dog could be taken at the airport? Here's what triggers it and how to prevent it. |
| 2 | Official source cited | Link the official/government source for each claim. | DON'T: Dubai requires certain documents. &rarr; DO: Dubai requires an import permit — see MOCCAE (moccae.gov.ae). |
| 3 | Specific route / variant named | Name the specific route/variant (e.g. 'Dubai to UK'). | DON'T: We cover all international routes. &rarr; DO: Dubai to UK: titer test, 4-month wait, DEFRA rules. |
| 4 | Step-by-step process | Add a numbered step-by-step process with timings. | DON'T: We handle everything for you. &rarr; DO: 1. Microchip 2. Rabies vaccine 3. Titer test 4. Import permit 5. Fly. |
| 5 | Timeline included | State the real timeline in days/weeks/months. | DON'T: Quick and easy relocation. &rarr; DO: Plan 3–6 months: titer results take 21–28 days; the permit is valid 90 days. |
| 6 | Cost ranges shown | Show real cost ranges (numbers), not 'contact us for a quote'. | DON'T: Contact us for a quote. &rarr; DO: Titer test 700–1,300 AED (no official price); release fee 500 AED (MOCCAE). |
| 7 | Common-mistakes section | Add a 'common mistakes to avoid' section. | DON'T: (no mistakes section) &rarr; DO: Common mistake: booking the flight before the titer results are back. |
| 8 | Original visuals (not stock) | Replace stock photos with real photos of the actual work. | DON'T: A stock photo of a smiling dog. &rarr; DO: A real photo of the actual crate prep and airport handover. |
| 9 | CTA feels like help | Offer something useful (a checklist/guide) before asking for the enquiry. | DON'T: Get a Quote &rarr; DO: Download the Dubai pet-import checklist (verified vs MOCCAE 2026). |
| 10 | Proof beside every claim | Put proof beside each claim, not only in a testimonials block. | DON'T: A testimonials block at the bottom only. &rarr; DO: A MOCCAE screenshot beside the 500 AED fee claim. |


**Run:** `python skill-engines/trust-gap/engine.py --url <URL>` or `--serve 8091` (web checker).
