# Specific route / variant named

*Measurement 3 of Trust Gap Analysis.*

## What it measures (how)
We check whether the page names a specific route or variant ('Dubai to UK') vs. 'all routes'.

## Why it works
Specificity signals real experience. 'We handle all international routes' reads as a brochure; a named route reads as someone who has actually done it.

## What to do (the fix)
Name the specific route/variant (e.g. 'Dubai to UK').

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: We cover all international routes.
- DO: Dubai to UK: titer test, 4-month wait, DEFRA rules.
