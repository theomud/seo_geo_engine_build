# Fear named in first 100 words

*Measurement 1 of Trust Gap Analysis.*

## What it measures (how)
We read the first ~120 words and look for the visitor's actual concern in their language.

## Why it works
A frightened buyer decides in seconds whether you 'get' them. Naming the fear first earns trust; opening with 'Welcome to our company' loses them.

## What to do (the fix)
Name the customer's real fear in the first 100 words.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Welcome — your trusted pet partner.
- DO: Worried your dog could be taken at the airport? Here's what triggers it and how to prevent it.
