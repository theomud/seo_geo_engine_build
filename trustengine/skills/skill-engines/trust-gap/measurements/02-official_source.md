# Official source cited

*Measurement 2 of Trust Gap Analysis.*

## What it measures (how)
We look for links to government/authority sites (.gov, .edu, regulators) and citation phrases.

## Why it works
In high-stakes markets a claim with no official source is just an assertion. A cited regulation is the difference between 'trust me' and 'here's the proof'.

## What to do (the fix)
Link the official/government source for each claim.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Dubai requires certain documents.
- DO: Dubai requires an import permit — see MOCCAE (moccae.gov.ae).
