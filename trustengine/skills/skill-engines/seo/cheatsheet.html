SEO Engine — Cheatsheet

Organic-ranking readiness — Topicality, Quality/Authority, Technical (the Google axis).

# Measurement What to do Example (don't → do)
1 Topicality (T*) · intent match Make the page directly answer its target query. DON'T: Page rambles off-topic. → DO: Page directly answers its target query.
2 Topicality (T*) · body depth Add depth — entities and quantified facts, not fluff. DON'T: 300 words of fluff. → DO: Depth with entities + quantified facts.
3 Topicality (T*) · internal anchors Use descriptive internal anchor text. —
4 Topicality (T*) · inbound anchors (needs a backlink data source) —
5 Topicality (T*) · title meta signal Write a compelling, accurate title + meta description. DON'T: Untitled / 'Home'. → DO: Accurate, compelling title + meta description.
6 Topicality (T*) · topical focus Keep the page and site focused on the topic. —
7 Quality & Authority (Q*) · eeat author Add a credentialed, named author. DON'T: No author named. → DO: By a named, credentialed author + Person schema.
8 Quality & Authority (Q*) · trust signals Add HTTPS, privacy, terms, contact and an about page. DON'T: No HTTPS / no contact. → DO: HTTPS, privacy, terms, contact, about.
9 Quality & Authority (Q*) · business legit Add Organization schema + a consistent verifiable NAP. —
10 Quality & Authority (Q*) · inbound authority (needs a backlink data source) —
11 Quality & Authority (Q*) · ymyl handling Add sourcing and disclaimers for YMYL topics. —
12 Quality & Authority (Q*) · brand demand (needs analytics/SERP data) —
13 Technical & Page Experience · indexability Ensure no accidental noindex; set the canonical correctly. DON'T: Accidental noindex. → DO: Indexable with a correct canonical.
14 Technical & Page Experience · core web vitals Cut page weight and render-blocking scripts. DON'T: 4MB of blocking scripts. → DO: Lean page, no render-blocking.
15 Technical & Page Experience · mobile friendly Add a responsive viewport meta. DON'T: No viewport meta. → DO: Responsive viewport + tap targets.
16 Technical & Page Experience · structured data Add valid schema (Organization/Article/FAQ). DON'T: No schema. → DO: Valid Organization/Article schema.
17 Technical & Page Experience · site architecture Use shallow URLs and internal linking. —
18 Technical & Page Experience · crawl hygiene Add an XML sitemap and a clean robots.txt. DON'T: No sitemap, messy robots. → DO: XML sitemap + clean robots.txt.
