# Scannable lists & tables

*Measurement 7 of AI Citation Readiness.*

## What it measures (how)
We count lists and tables — structured data points an engine can lift cleanly.

## Why it works
Lists and tables are the most extractable format on the web; a comparison table or step list is often quoted wholesale into an AI answer.

## What to do (the fix)
Add lists and a comparison table for the key facts.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: We cover the EU, Australia and more.
- DO: A table: EU — 30 days | Australia — 180 days.
