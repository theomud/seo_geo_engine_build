# Statistics & specific numbers

*Measurement 2 of AI Citation Readiness.*

## What it measures (how)
We count the specific quantified facts on the page — percentages, prices, dates, counts, durations.

## Why it works
Adding statistics is one of the strongest citation levers measured (~+24.9%). A concrete number ('500 AED', '90 days', '66%') is exactly the kind of fact an engine quotes verbatim.

## What to do (the fix)
Add specific numbers, %, prices and dated figures to the page.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: We move many pets safely.
- DO: 256,114 pets were transported in 2021, with an incident rate below 1 in 10,000.
