# Cited sources

*Measurement 3 of AI Citation Readiness.*

## What it measures (how)
We look for citation phrases ('according to', 'source:', 'per …') and outbound links to authoritative domains (.gov, .edu, .org).

## Why it works
Citing sources is the single biggest citation lever (~+27.8%). Engines prefer claims that are backed by an authority over claims that just assert themselves.

## What to do (the fix)
Cite authoritative sources (link .gov/.edu/official pages; use 'according to…').

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Pets need vaccinations.
- DO: Per MOCCAE, pets need a microchip, rabies vaccination and import permit (moccae.gov.ae).
