# Answer-first opening

*Measurement 1 of AI Citation Readiness.*

## What it measures (how)
We read the first ~120 words and check whether the page answers the question immediately (a definition, a number, a direct statement) instead of warming up with an intro.

## Why it works
AI engines lift the opening of a page. If your answer is buried under 'Welcome to our company', the engine lifts a competitor's answer instead. Answer-first = liftable.

## What to do (the fix)
Put a direct answer (a number or definition) in the first 100 words.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Welcome to our pet relocation company.
- DO: A dog is held at Dubai airport when its rabies titer paperwork is incomplete; the release fee is 500 AED.
