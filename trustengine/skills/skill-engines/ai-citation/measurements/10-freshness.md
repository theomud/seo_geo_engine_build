# Freshness & dating

*Measurement 10 of AI Citation Readiness.*

## What it measures (how)
We check for recent years and dating language ('updated', 'published', a visible date).

## Why it works
On topics that change, engines favour current sources. A visible recent date signals the fact is still good; an undated page reads as possibly stale.

## What to do (the fix)
Add a visible published/updated date and a recent year.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: © 2019 (or no date)
- DO: Updated June 2026 — verified against current MOCCAE rules.
