# Alt text coverage

*Measurement 2 of Visual Evidence Architecture.*

## What it measures (how)
We measure the share of images with alt text.

## Why it works
Alt text makes images accessible and machine-readable — and signals real, described evidence.

## What to do (the fix)
Add descriptive alt text to every image.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: <img> with no alt attribute.
- DO: alt="MOCCAE import-fee page, captured 2026-06".
