# Video / rich media

*Measurement 5 of Visual Evidence Architecture.*

## What it measures (how)
We detect video tags and YouTube/Vimeo embeds.

## Why it works
Video is a strong trust + citation surface, especially for process/how-to content.

## What to do (the fix)
Add a short video (process/explainer) or transcript-backed embed.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: No video.
- DO: A 60-second process video with its transcript on the page.
