# Images present

*Measurement 1 of Visual Evidence Architecture.*

## What it measures (how)
We count images on the page.

## Why it works
A page of pure text gives a wary visitor nothing to believe; visuals carry proof.

## What to do (the fix)
Add real images that show the work/proof.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: A wall of text, no images.
- DO: Real photos of the crate prep, vet check and airport handover.
