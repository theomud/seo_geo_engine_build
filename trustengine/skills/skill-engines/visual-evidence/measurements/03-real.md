# Real (non-stock) images

*Measurement 3 of Visual Evidence Architecture.*

## What it measures (how)
We flag images whose source looks like a stock library.

## Why it works
Stock 'happy customer' photos are tuned out; a real photo of the actual work is proof.

## What to do (the fix)
Replace stock photos with real photos of the actual work.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: A stock smiling-dog photo.
- DO: Your own photo of the actual handover.
