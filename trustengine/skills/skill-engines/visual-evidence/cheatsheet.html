Visual Evidence Architecture — Cheatsheet

Does the page SHOW proof — real photos, dated screenshots, data visuals — beside its claims?

# Measurement What to do Example (don't → do)
1 Images present Add real images that show the work/proof. DON'T: A wall of text, no images. → DO: Real photos of the crate prep, vet check and airport handover.
2 Alt text coverage Add descriptive alt text to every image. DON'T: with no alt attribute. → DO: alt="MOCCAE import-fee page, captured 2026-06".
3 Real (non-stock) images Replace stock photos with real photos of the actual work. DON'T: A stock smiling-dog photo. → DO: Your own photo of the actual handover.
4 Data visuals (tables/charts) Turn key numbers into a table or simple chart/infographic. DON'T: Costs written in a paragraph. → DO: A cost table or a simple bar chart.
5 Video / rich media Add a short video (process/explainer) or transcript-backed embed. DON'T: No video. → DO: A 60-second process video with its transcript on the page.
6 Captions / figure context Wrap images in DON'T: A bare image. → DO: .
7 Visual density Add a proof image beside each major claim, not just one at the top. DON'T: One hero image only. → DO: A proof image beside each major claim.
