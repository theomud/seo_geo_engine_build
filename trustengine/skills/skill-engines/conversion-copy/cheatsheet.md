# Conversion Copy — Cheatsheet

*Does the copy convert a worried reader — fear-acknowledging headline, opening, and a help-first CTA?*

| # | Measurement | What to do | Example (don't &rarr; do) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fear-acknowledging opening | Open with the customer's real fear in their words, then resolve it. | DON'T: Welcome to our company. &rarr; DO: Worried your dog could be taken at the airport? Here's how to prevent it. |
| 2 | Headline from the resolved fear | Rewrite the H1 as the question they're asking or the answer they want. | DON'T: Pet Relocation Services in Dubai. &rarr; DO: What happens if your dog is taken at Dubai airport — and the document that stops it. |
| 3 | Help-first CTA | Offer a useful free thing (checklist/guide) before asking for the enquiry. | DON'T: Get a Quote. &rarr; DO: Download the airport checklist (free). |
| 4 | Social proof | Add real reviews/ratings or a specific client count. | DON'T: (no reviews shown) &rarr; DO: Rated 4.8/5 across 283 reviews. |
| 5 | Objections answered | Add an FAQ that answers the top objections directly. | DON'T: (no FAQ) &rarr; DO: FAQ: "Can pets fly to Dubai in summer?" |
| 6 | Specific, not vague | Replace vague claims with real numbers and specifics. | DON'T: Affordable, reliable service. &rarr; DO: From 2,500 AED; realistic 3–6 month timeline. |
