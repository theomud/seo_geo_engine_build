# Headline from the resolved fear

*Measurement 2 of Conversion Copy.*

## What it measures (how)
We check the H1 for a question/benefit vs a generic 'Services/Welcome'.

## Why it works
The headline is the ad for the page; a fear/benefit headline pulls the reader in, a brochure headline loses them.

## What to do (the fix)
Rewrite the H1 as the question they're asking or the answer they want.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Pet Relocation Services in Dubai.
- DO: What happens if your dog is taken at Dubai airport — and the document that stops it.
