# Social proof

*Measurement 4 of Conversion Copy.*

## What it measures (how)
We count reviews/ratings/testimonials and client-count signals.

## Why it works
People follow others under uncertainty; visible proof others trusted you reduces the perceived risk.

## What to do (the fix)
Add real reviews/ratings or a specific client count.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: (no reviews shown)
- DO: Rated 4.8/5 across 283 reviews.
