# Help-first CTA

*Measurement 3 of Conversion Copy.*

## What it measures (how)
We compare help offers (download/checklist/guide) vs pure asks (get a quote).

## Why it works
Giving value before asking lowers the guard; 'Get a quote' asks the scared visitor to take a risk first.

## What to do (the fix)
Offer a useful free thing (checklist/guide) before asking for the enquiry.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Get a Quote.
- DO: Download the airport checklist (free).
