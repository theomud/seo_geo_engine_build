# Customer Fear Intelligence — Cheatsheet

*Does the page speak to the customer's real fears and the questions they actually ask?*

| # | Measurement | What to do | Example (don't &rarr; do) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fear acknowledged first | Open by naming the customer's real fear in their words. | DON'T: We are a pet relocation company. &rarr; DO: Worried your dog won't be allowed in? Here's what actually happens. |
| 2 | Range of fears covered | Address the other fears this customer has, not just one. | DON'T: Only mentions cost. &rarr; DO: Covers confiscation, documents, timeline and the summer embargo. |
| 3 | Real questions answered | Turn the real questions people ask into on-page Q&A. | DON'T: (no questions) &rarr; DO: How much does the titer test cost? Can pets fly in summer? |
| 4 | Objections / FAQ | Add an FAQ that answers the top objections. | DON'T: (no FAQ) &rarr; DO: FAQ: "Is cargo safe for my dog?" |
| 5 | Real customer voice | Use a real customer quote / the words customers actually use. | DON'T: Brochure copy. &rarr; DO: Owners say: "I was terrified it would be taken at the airport." |
| 6 | Reassurance | Add reassurance ('here's exactly what to do') alongside each fear. | DON'T: (none) &rarr; DO: You're not alone — here's exactly what to do, step by step. |


**Run:** `python skill-engines/customer-fear-intelligence/engine.py --url <URL>` or `--serve 8099` (web checker).
