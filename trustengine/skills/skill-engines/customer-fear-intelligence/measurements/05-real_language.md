# Real customer voice

*Measurement 5 of Customer Fear Intelligence.*

## What it measures (how)
We look for quotes / 'owners say' real language.

## Why it works
Real customer words resonate; brochure language reads as out-of-touch.

## What to do (the fix)
Use a real customer quote / the words customers actually use.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Brochure copy.
- DO: Owners say: "I was terrified it would be taken at the airport."
