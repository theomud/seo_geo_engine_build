# Reassurance

*Measurement 6 of Customer Fear Intelligence.*

## What it measures (how)
We look for reassurance phrasing.

## Why it works
Naming a fear without reassurance creates avoidance; reassurance turns fear into action.

## What to do (the fix)
Add reassurance ('here's exactly what to do') alongside each fear.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: (none)
- DO: You're not alone — here's exactly what to do, step by step.
