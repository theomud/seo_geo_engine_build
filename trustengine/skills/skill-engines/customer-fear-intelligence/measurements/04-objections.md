# Objections / FAQ

*Measurement 4 of Customer Fear Intelligence.*

## What it measures (how)
We look for FAQ/objection-handling.

## Why it works
An unanswered objection is a silent exit; addressing it removes the reason to leave.

## What to do (the fix)
Add an FAQ that answers the top objections.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: (no FAQ)
- DO: FAQ: "Is cargo safe for my dog?"
