# Range of fears covered

*Measurement 2 of Customer Fear Intelligence.*

## What it measures (how)
We count distinct fear/concern terms across the page.

## Why it works
Customers carry several fears; covering the cluster keeps them engaged and builds trust.

## What to do (the fix)
Address the other fears this customer has, not just one.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Only mentions cost.
- DO: Covers confiscation, documents, timeline and the summer embargo.
