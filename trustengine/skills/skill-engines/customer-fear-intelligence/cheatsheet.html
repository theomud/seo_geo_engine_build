Customer Fear Intelligence — Cheatsheet

Does the page speak to the customer's real fears and the questions they actually ask?

# Measurement What to do Example (don't → do)
1 Fear acknowledged first Open by naming the customer's real fear in their words. DON'T: We are a pet relocation company. → DO: Worried your dog won't be allowed in? Here's what actually happens.
2 Range of fears covered Address the other fears this customer has, not just one. DON'T: Only mentions cost. → DO: Covers confiscation, documents, timeline and the summer embargo.
3 Real questions answered Turn the real questions people ask into on-page Q&A. DON'T: (no questions) → DO: How much does the titer test cost? Can pets fly in summer?
4 Objections / FAQ Add an FAQ that answers the top objections. DON'T: (no FAQ) → DO: FAQ: "Is cargo safe for my dog?"
5 Real customer voice Use a real customer quote / the words customers actually use. DON'T: Brochure copy. → DO: Owners say: "I was terrified it would be taken at the airport."
6 Reassurance Add reassurance ('here's exactly what to do') alongside each fear. DON'T: (none) → DO: You're not alone — here's exactly what to do, step by step.
