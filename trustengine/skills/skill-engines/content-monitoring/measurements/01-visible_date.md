# Visible date

*Measurement 1 of Content Monitoring (freshness).*

## What it measures (how)
We look for a published/updated date (text or schema).

## Why it works
A visible recent date tells readers and engines the fact is current.

## What to do (the fix)
Add a visible 'last updated' date (and dateModified schema).

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: (no date)
- DO: Last updated: June 2026.
