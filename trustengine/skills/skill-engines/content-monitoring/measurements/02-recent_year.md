# Recent-year reference

*Measurement 2 of Content Monitoring (freshness).*

## What it measures (how)
We check for a recent year (≥2025).

## Why it works
On volatile topics, recency is a ranking and trust signal.

## What to do (the fix)
Refresh facts and reference the current year.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: facts cite 2021
- DO: rules current for 2026.
