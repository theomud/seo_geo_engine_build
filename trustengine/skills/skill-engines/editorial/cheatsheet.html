# Measurement What to do Example (don't → do)
1 Specific (real numbers & names) Add real numbers and named examples; cut generic claims. DON'T: We deliver exceptional results. → DO: We completed 450 moves in 2025 with a 99.6% on-time rate.
2 Clear & scannable Break into subheadings and lists; shorten over-long paragraphs. DON'T: One 400-word paragraph. → DO: Three H2 sections + a 5-step list.
3 Proof / trustworthy Back each claim with a number, source, or citation. DON'T: We are the best provider. → DO: Rated 4.8/5 across 283 reviews.
4 Clear call-to-action Add one clear call-to-action. DON'T: (no call-to-action) → DO: Download the checklist.
5 No filler opening Delete the filler opening; start with the reader's problem or a fact. DON'T: In today's fast-paced world, pet travel is complex. → DO: Moving a pet to Dubai takes 3–6 months. Here's the timeline.
6 No unsupported superlatives Replace 'leading/best' superlatives with a proof point, or cut them. DON'T: The leading pet relocation company. → DO: IPATA-accredited since 2018 (member #1234).
7 No vague jargon Cut buzzwords (innovative solutions, synergy, leverage, seamless). DON'T: We leverage innovative solutions for seamless relocations. → DO: We handle the permit, the titer test and the flight booking.
8 No fake enthusiasm Remove hype words and excess exclamation marks; show, don't gush. DON'T: We are absolutely passionate about amazing results!!! → DO: We've moved 450 pets; here's how the process works.
9 Strong ending (no dead end) End with a clear next step, not 'thanks for visiting'. DON'T: Thanks for visiting! → DO: Next step: download the checklist and send us your flight date.
