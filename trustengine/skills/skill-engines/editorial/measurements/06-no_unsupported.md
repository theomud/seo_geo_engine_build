# No unsupported superlatives

*Measurement 6 of Editorial Quality.*

## What it measures (how)
We look for 'leading / best / #1 / world-class' without nearby proof.

## Why it works
An unbacked 'the leading provider' reads as empty marketing and erodes trust. Prove it or cut it.

## What to do (the fix)
Replace 'leading/best' superlatives with a proof point, or cut them.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: The leading pet relocation company.
- DO: IPATA-accredited since 2018 (member #1234).
