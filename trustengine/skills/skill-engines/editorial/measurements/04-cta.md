# Clear call-to-action

*Measurement 4 of Editorial Quality.*

## What it measures (how)
We check for a clear next step / call-to-action.

## Why it works
Content with no next step is a dead end. One clear action turns a reader into a lead.

## What to do (the fix)
Add one clear call-to-action.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: (no call-to-action)
- DO: Download the checklist.
