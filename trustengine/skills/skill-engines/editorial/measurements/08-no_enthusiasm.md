# No fake enthusiasm

*Measurement 8 of Editorial Quality.*

## What it measures (how)
We flag hype words ('passionate', 'amazing', 'exceptional') and exclamation-mark overuse.

## Why it works
Manufactured excitement reads as insincere. Show passion through specifics, not adjectives.

## What to do (the fix)
Remove hype words and excess exclamation marks; show, don't gush.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: We are absolutely passionate about amazing results!!!
- DO: We've moved 450 pets; here's how the process works.
