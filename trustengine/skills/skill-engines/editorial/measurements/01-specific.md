# Specific (real numbers & names)

*Measurement 1 of Editorial Quality.*

## What it measures (how)
We count concrete numbers and named entities per 500 words.

## Why it works
Specifics are what make content credible and un-generic. 'We deliver results' applies to anyone; '256,114 pets moved in 2021' could only come from someone who did the work.

## What to do (the fix)
Add real numbers and named examples; cut generic claims.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: We deliver exceptional results.
- DO: We completed 450 moves in 2025 with a 99.6% on-time rate.
