# Clear & scannable

*Measurement 2 of Editorial Quality.*

## What it measures (how)
We count subheadings, lists and tables, and flag over-long paragraphs.

## Why it works
A reader decides in ~10 seconds. Structure lets them find the answer; a wall of text loses them.

## What to do (the fix)
Break into subheadings and lists; shorten over-long paragraphs.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: One 400-word paragraph.
- DO: Three H2 sections + a 5-step list.
