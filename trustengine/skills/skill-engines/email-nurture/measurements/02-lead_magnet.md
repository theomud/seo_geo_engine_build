# Lead magnet

*Measurement 2 of Email Nurture (capture readiness).*

## What it measures (how)
We look for a downloadable free offer.

## Why it works
A useful free thing is what makes a worried visitor hand over their email.

## What to do (the fix)
Offer a free checklist/guide in exchange for an email.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Nothing to download.
- DO: Free Dubai pet-import checklist (email to get it).
