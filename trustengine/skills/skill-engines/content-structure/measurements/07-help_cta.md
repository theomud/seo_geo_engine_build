# Layer 5 — help-first CTA

*Measurement 7 of Content Structure for Trust.*

## What it measures (how)
We compare help-first offers (download/checklist/guide) against pure asks (get a quote).

## Why it works
Offering value before asking lowers the guard; 'Get a quote' asks the scared visitor to take a risk first.

## What to do (the fix)
End with a help-first CTA (checklist/guide), not 'get a quote'.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Request a consultation.
- DO: Get the one-page airport checklist.
