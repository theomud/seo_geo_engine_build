# Layer 3 — proof / evidence

*Measurement 5 of Content Structure for Trust.*

## What it measures (how)
We count images and proof references (official sources).

## Why it works
Proof beside the claim is believed; an unbacked claim is not — especially in high-stakes niches.

## What to do (the fix)
Add proof (official-source screenshot / real images) beside claims.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: A stock image.
- DO: A MOCCAE screenshot beside the rule it proves.
