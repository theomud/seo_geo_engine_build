# Layer 2 — direct answer in the opening

*Measurement 2 of Content Structure for Trust.*

## What it measures (how)
We check the opening for a direct answer (a number, a definition, a yes/no).

## Why it works
After the fear, the page must resolve it immediately. A buried answer loses the anxious reader.

## What to do (the fix)
Give the direct answer immediately after naming the fear.

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: A long company intro before any answer.
- DO: Yes, pets can enter Dubai year-round, but airlines suspend cargo in summer heat.
