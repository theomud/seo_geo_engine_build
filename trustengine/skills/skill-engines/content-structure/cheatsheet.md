# Content Structure for Trust — Cheatsheet

*Is the page built around the visitor's fear, in the 5 layers that convert?*

| # | Measurement | What to do | Example (don't &rarr; do) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Layer 1 — fear acknowledged first | Open the first 100 words on the customer's fear, in their words. | DON'T: About our company. &rarr; DO: If you've been told your dog could be taken at Dubai airport — that fear is real. Here's how to prevent it. |
| 2 | Layer 2 — direct answer in the opening | Give the direct answer immediately after naming the fear. | DON'T: A long company intro before any answer. &rarr; DO: Yes, pets can enter Dubai year-round, but airlines suspend cargo in summer heat. |
| 3 | Layer 2 — verified fact early | Add a verified, cited fact near the top. | DON'T: It costs a lot. &rarr; DO: The release fee is 500 AED per dog (MOCCAE, verified 2026). |
| 4 | Layer 3 — process / steps | Add the step-by-step process. | DON'T: We take care of the whole process. &rarr; DO: Step 1 microchip … Step 5 fly. |
| 5 | Layer 3 — proof / evidence | Add proof (official-source screenshot / real images) beside claims. | DON'T: A stock image. &rarr; DO: A MOCCAE screenshot beside the rule it proves. |
| 6 | Layer 4 — related fears linked | Link 2–3 related-fear pages internally. | DON'T: A dead-end page with no links. &rarr; DO: Links to the 'summer embargo' and 'documentation mistakes' pages. |
| 7 | Layer 5 — help-first CTA | End with a help-first CTA (checklist/guide), not 'get a quote'. | DON'T: Request a consultation. &rarr; DO: Get the one-page airport checklist. |
| 8 | Layered heading structure | Add H2/H3 headings to segment the page into the 5 layers. | DON'T: A wall of text. &rarr; DO: H2: 'What triggers confiscation' → 'The 3 steps that prevent it'. |


**Run:** `python skill-engines/content-structure/engine.py --url <URL>` or `--serve 8093` (web checker).
