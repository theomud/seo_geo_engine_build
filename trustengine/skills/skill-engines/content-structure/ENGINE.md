## MODELS — the data structures and mental models this skill uses

- **M-07 — Five-Layer Page Model.** Fear acknowledgement → verified answer → process/evidence → related fears → help-first CTA; trust built in sequence. *Source:* corroborated by NN/g Hierarchy of Trust.
- **M-18 — NN/g Hierarchy of Trust** (5 commitment levels). The parent model of which M-07 is this skill's operational instance. *Source:* Aurora Harley, Nielsen Norman Group.
- **M-19 — Nielsen's 4 Credibility Factors** (design quality, up-front disclosure, comprehensive/current content, connection to the rest of the web). Governs why Layer 3 evidence must be real. *Source:* Jakob Nielsen 1999 / NN/g 2016.

## FRAMEWORKS — the decision systems and processes

- **F-08 — Proof Interstitial Framework.** Proof sits beside each claim throughout the page, not in one block. *Source:* internal (corroborated by Fogg et al. 2003 credibility study).
- **F-16 — Content Architecture Hierarchy.** Page type (9 types) × content depth (4 levels) × service/location position; the structural scaffold for every page. *Source:* internal.
- **F-31 — "Who / How / Why" Self-Assessment Loop.** Each page answers who wrote it, how it was verified, and why it serves the reader. *Source:* Google Search Central, people-first content.

## PRINCIPLES — the non-negotiable rules

- **P-03 — Proof Over Promise.** Every factual claim cites a Verified Source Bank row; Unverifiable claims are hedged, never asserted. *Source:* Fogg et al. (2003), DUX '03 (n=2,684).
- **P-05 — One Fear Per Page.** Each page resolves exactly one primary fear; related fears get their own pages + Layer-4 links. *Source:* Protection Motivation Theory (one threat-coping pair per appeal).
- **P-39 — Replace Stock Heroes with Real Customer/Team Photos.** Layer-3 evidence is real photos and official-source screenshots, never stock. *Source:* NN/g eye-tracking via CXL.
