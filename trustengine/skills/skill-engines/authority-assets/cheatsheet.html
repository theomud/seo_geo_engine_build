# Measurement What to do Example (don't → do)
1 Named, credentialed author Add a named author with real credentials (+ Person schema). DON'T: Anonymous page. → DO: By Jane Doe, IPATA-certified, 8 years' experience.
2 Proof density Add more verifiable proof (figures, citations) beside claims — aim ≥1 per 200 words. DON'T: General claims throughout. → DO: A figure or citation every couple of sentences.
3 Documented case / real example Document one real case end-to-end, including what went wrong. DON'T: We help many clients. → DO: In 2025 we moved a French Bulldog London→Dubai; the titer nearly lapsed — here's what we did.
4 Original data / statistics Publish an original statistic from your own data. DON'T: (no data) → DO: 74% of failed moves were due to incomplete titer docs, per our 450-move dataset.
5 Cited sources Cite the primary/official source for each factual claim. DON'T: Pets need vaccines. → DO: Per MOCCAE (moccae.gov.ae).
6 Credentials / accreditation Show your licences/accreditations (IPATA, IATA, registration). DON'T: (none shown) → DO: IPATA member #1234, IATA-certified.
7 Un-AI-able specificity Add specific names, places, figures only someone who did the work would know. DON'T: We cover international routes. → DO: Dubai→Heathrow via Emirates cargo; summer embargo Jun–Sep.
