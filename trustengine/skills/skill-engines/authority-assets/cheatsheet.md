# Authority Asset Creation — Cheatsheet

*Could only someone who did the real work have written this — or could any AI have?*

| # | Measurement | What to do | Example (don't &rarr; do) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Named, credentialed author | Add a named author with real credentials (+ Person schema). | DON'T: Anonymous page. &rarr; DO: By Jane Doe, IPATA-certified, 8 years' experience. |
| 2 | Proof density | Add more verifiable proof (figures, citations) beside claims — aim ≥1 per 200 words. | DON'T: General claims throughout. &rarr; DO: A figure or citation every couple of sentences. |
| 3 | Documented case / real example | Document one real case end-to-end, including what went wrong. | DON'T: We help many clients. &rarr; DO: In 2025 we moved a French Bulldog London→Dubai; the titer nearly lapsed — here's what we did. |
| 4 | Original data / statistics | Publish an original statistic from your own data. | DON'T: (no data) &rarr; DO: 74% of failed moves were due to incomplete titer docs, per our 450-move dataset. |
| 5 | Cited sources | Cite the primary/official source for each factual claim. | DON'T: Pets need vaccines. &rarr; DO: Per MOCCAE (moccae.gov.ae). |
| 6 | Credentials / accreditation | Show your licences/accreditations (IPATA, IATA, registration). | DON'T: (none shown) &rarr; DO: IPATA member #1234, IATA-certified. |
| 7 | Un-AI-able specificity | Add specific names, places, figures only someone who did the work would know. | DON'T: We cover international routes. &rarr; DO: Dubai→Heathrow via Emirates cargo; summer embargo Jun–Sep. |


**Run:** `python skill-engines/authority-assets/engine.py --url <URL>` or `--serve 8096` (web checker).
