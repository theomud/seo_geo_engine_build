# Un-AI-able specificity

*Measurement 7 of Authority Asset Creation.*

## What it measures (how)
We measure named-entity density (concrete, specific detail).

## Why it works
Specifics a generic prompt can't produce are the signature of real, authored work.

## What to do (the fix)
