# Named, credentialed author

*Measurement 1 of Authority Asset Creation.*

## What it measures (how)
We look for a byline, Person schema, and credential language.

## Why it works
AI content is anonymous; a verifiable expert author is exactly what E-E-A-T and readers trust.

## What to do (the fix)
Add a named author with real credentials (+ Person schema).

## Example (what NOT to do -> what to do)
- DON'T: Anonymous page.
- DO: By Jane Doe, IPATA-certified, 8 years' experience.
