## PHASE 1 — MARKET RESEARCH ✅ DONE

**What was researched:** why fact-correct, well-structured pages still fail to convert in a high-fear market; how "salesy" copy repels a frightened buyer; what fear-acknowledging copy actually requires.
**Key findings:** conversion is won on the **actual words** — headline, opening, CTA — not architecture or facts alone. In a regulated market hype repels; the writing that converts **names the fear and resolves it with a verified answer**, then offers help. The method reduces to **three moves** with two inputs (named fear + verified fact).
**Sources / library:** M-21 Cialdini, M-20 Protection Motivation Theory, M-02 Fear Hierarchy; F-19 PAS, F-18 AIDA, F-20 AICPBSAWN; P-04 Fear-Acknowledging, P-11 Real Examples, P-13 Hormozi.
**Verdict:** high-commercial, teachable; fear-acknowledging copy as a scorable discipline is the asset.

**What failed or surprised (the real catch):** the market's titer-cost page — and what an unguarded AI writes — asserts a **fabricated single price as fact** ("the titer test costs 700 AED", uncited). In this niche a wrong number gets a pet refused, so that is harm, not a typo. The honest rewrite *removes* the number ("no official price; owners pay ~700–1,300 AED, get it in writing", C-001 hedge). The surprise: we expected honesty to convert **worse** — it scored **higher** (48/50). Refusing to invent a number is the conversion move.

**Verdict:** the three moves work end-to-end; the P-04 "resolve in the same breath" rule and the cite-or-hedge rule are what keep the copy acknowledging, not exploiting.

## WHAT THIS SKILL PROVED

**Core finding:** fear-acknowledging copy is a **scorable discipline**, not a talent — the voice /5, the help-first CTA test, and the cite-or-hedge rule make "good copy" objective and reproducible. The clearest proof: honesty about an unknown number **out-converts** a confident fake one, because this buyer has been "quoted endless amount" and trusts the page that refuses to do it again.
**What changed from the spec:** two pages (embargo, airport comparison) meet the copy threshold but carry honest "verify this fact before going live" flags — a publishing dependency surfaced by the source map, not a copy defect.
**What others don't do:** no off-the-shelf copy method ties every emotional claim to a named real quote and every number to a Source Bank C-ID (or an honest hedge) in a market where wrong information causes real harm.
