# Skill · File 01 — What This Skill Is
## Niche-agnostic definition of Conversion Copy

---

## The problem this skill solves

A page can pass every other check — true facts, sound structure, clean grammar —
and still convert nobody. The reader arrives **afraid** (they are about to move a
living animal across a border where one paperwork error means refusal at the
counter) and the page talks to them in the flat, corporate voice of a brochure.
They leave and ask a Facebook group instead.

Conversion Copy is the skill that writes the **actual words** that close that gap:
the headline, the opening that says the fear out loud in the reader's own language,
and the call-to-action that offers help before it asks for anything. It is the
difference between a page that *informs* and a page that *converts* — without ever
tipping into hype, which this buyer distrusts on sight.

---

## What this skill produces

| Output | What it is |
|--------|-----------|
| Headlines | The H1 / key sub-heads that name the fear and promise a verified answer |
| Fear-acknowledging openings | The first 1–3 sentences — the fear said plainly, in customer words, then resolved |
| Help-first CTAs | The close — a genuinely useful offer given *before* the enquiry is asked for |

Each is scored: **40+/50** on the editorial rubric, **4+/5** on in-the-trenches voice,
and the CTA must pass the *"would you rather receive this or get a quote?"* test.

---

## The three moves (the whole method)

**1. Name the fear in their words, not yours.** *(Library: P-04 Fear-Acknowledging
Not Fear-Exploiting; M-02 Fear Hierarchy.)* Copy is pulled from real community
language, not invented. The deepest fear in the proof niche is not "import
compliance" — it is Muze Gu's: *"without it your dog will be taken away in airport
and never give back."* That sentence is the opening, lightly cleaned, because no
marketer would write anything truer.

**2. Resolve it with a verified answer, immediately.** *(Library: M-01 Verified
Fact; F-19 PAS — Problem, Agitate, **Solution**.)* PMT is the load-bearing model
here: a fear stated **without** a credible resolution produces denial and
avoidance, not action (M-20). So every opening pairs the named fear with a
Source-Bank-cited fact (by C-ID) or an honest hedge — never a bare scare.

**3. Close with help, not pressure.** *(Library: P-13 Hormozi Test; Cialdini
Reciprocity, M-21.)* The CTA offers the thing the scared owner actually wants — the
real titer-cost breakdown, the embargo calendar, the airport-comparison table —
*before* asking for the enquiry. "Get a free quote" fails; "Get the one-page
checklist that stops your dog being turned away" passes.

---

## A worked example (the proof niche)

The four universal gap pages, each built from a real named fear:

| Page | The fear, in customer language | Resolved with |
|------|-------------------------------|---------------|
| Dog taken at the airport | *"taken away in airport and never give back"* (Muze Gu, FB) | The verified rejection/release process — MOCCAE-cited or honestly hedged |
| Move a pet in summer | *"before summer"* time-pressure; the embargo nobody warns about | The verified summer-embargo dates per airline |
| Rabies titer test cost | *"being quoted endless amount"* (7Ssisi, Reddit) | The real 700–1,300 AED range, sourced, not a single fake number |
| Sharjah vs Dubai vs Abu Dhabi | *"For paying so much money Etihad was a terrible experience"* (Reddit, 7↑) | The verified routing comparison, with the figures that exist |

A *competitor's* version of any of these opens with "Navigating the complexities of
pet relocation…" (a Filler Opening — fails Specific, fails voice) and closes with
"Contact us for a quote" (a Dead End that fails the help-first test). The contrast —
our fear-named, verified-answer, help-first page vs the market's brochure — is
exactly what this skill makes writable and scorable.

---

## How it differs from neighbouring skills

| Skill | Owns |
|-------|------|
| Content Structure | The **architecture** of the page — which layers go where |
| Editorial Judgment | **Whether** the writing is good enough — the /50 gate |
| **Conversion Copy** | The **actual words** — headline, opening, CTA — that convert |

Content Structure says "an opening that acknowledges the fear goes here."
Conversion Copy *writes that opening*. Editorial Judgment then scores it.

---

## Why this is a standalone skill

1. **The words are where conversion is won or lost.** Structure and facts can be
   perfect and the page still fails on a flat headline and a "Contact us" CTA.
2. **Fear-acknowledging copy is a teachable discipline, not a talent.** The three
   moves, the voice criterion, and the help-first CTA test make "good copy"
   objective and reproducible.
3. **It is niche-portable.** Any regulated, high-fear market (immigration, medical
   travel, licensing) has named fears and verifiable facts — the only two inputs.

---

## In scope / out of scope

**In scope.** Writing and rewriting headlines, fear-acknowledging openings, and
help-first CTAs from named fears + verified facts; scoring each against the
threshold; producing the before/after with a C-ID source map.

**Out of scope.** Discovering the fears (done upstream in fear intelligence),
verifying the facts (done upstream in source research), designing the page
architecture (content structure), and the final publish/hold decision (editorial
judgment). This skill writes the words; it does not source, verify, or arrange them.

---

## What "good" looks like

- Every gap page's copy scores **40+/50**; every opening scores **4+/5** on voice.
- Every fear-acknowledging opening traces to a **named real quote or a Column K
  fear** — never invented emotion.
- Every claim in the copy carries a **C-ID** from the Source Bank, or is honestly
  hedged. No bare scare, no unsourced number.
- Every CTA passes *"would you rather receive this or get a quote?"* — the offer is
  more useful to a frightened owner than a sales quote.

This skill is complete when the three moves, the voice criterion, and the
help-first CTA test exist for the proof niche, the four universal-gap pages have
been rewritten and scored at or above threshold, and the audit passes.
