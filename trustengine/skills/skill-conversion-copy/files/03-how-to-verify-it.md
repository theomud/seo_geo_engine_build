# Skill · File 03 — How To Verify It
## The gates that keep the copy honest, sourced, and fear-acknowledging — not fear-exploiting

---

## Why verification matters

Conversion copy fails in two directions, and both are dangerous in a high-fear
market. **Too soft:** brochure voice that converts nobody — a wasted page. **Too
hard:** copy that agitates the fear (your dog could be seized!) without resolving
it, or invents a number to force urgency. That second failure is worse than a weak
page: it exploits a frightened owner, breaches P-04, and in a market where wrong
information means a pet refused entry, it does real harm (P-02).

Verification checks three things: every emotional claim is **traceable to a real
fear**, every factual claim is **traceable to a C-ID or honestly hedged**, and an
**independent re-score agrees** the copy is fear-*acknowledging*, not exploiting.

---

## Gate set A — copy integrity (per page)

| Gate | Passes when |
|------|-------------|
| A1 · The fear is real, not invented | The opening's fear traces to a named quote or a Column K fear category — cited in the source map. An invented fear caps voice at 2. *(M-02)* |
| A2 · Fear is resolved in the same breath | Every agitated fear is answered immediately with a verified fact or honest hedge. Agitate-without-resolve = automatic fail, no matter the score. *(P-04)* |
| A3 · Every number is cited or hedged | Each factual claim carries a C-ID **or** is marked HEDGE with honest wording. An unsourced number (e.g. a single fake titer price) is cut. *(M-01, P-03)* |
| A4 · Voice is scored with a reason | The opening's voice /5 has a one-line reason naming the real language used. A bare number is not a score. |
| A5 · The CTA passes the help-first test | "Would you rather receive this or get a quote?" is answered, in writing, in favour of the offer. "Contact us for a quote" fails outright. *(P-13)* |

A2 and A5 are the two gates that catch the dangerous failures: A2 stops
fear-exploitation, A5 stops the Dead-End CTA. A page failing either is held, not
softened.

---

## Gate set B — the threshold check

The page clears the README's three-part Functional Quality Threshold:

1. **Editorial gate** — 40+/50 on the 10-criteria rubric, nothing below 3.
2. **Voice gate** — the fear-acknowledging opening scores **4+/5**.
3. **CTA gate** — the help-first test passes.

Any one unmet = the copy is not done. No averaging, no rounding a 39 up.

---

## The independent re-score (the core check)

A second scorer (a sub-agent or a different human) re-judges the page **blind to the
original scores**, seeing only: the copy, the named-fear source map, and the three
gates. Then compare:

- **The fear-acknowledging verdict is the hard test.** Both must agree the copy
  *resolves* the fear (acknowledging) rather than merely raising it (exploiting). A
  split here = the page is rewritten, never shipped.
- **Voice agreement:** the two voice scores should be within **±1 point**. A wider
  gap means "real customer language" is being judged inconsistently — reconcile
  against the named quote.
- **CTA agreement:** both must reach the same pass/fail on the help-first test.

---

## The audit sub-agent — verifying the verifier

After the batch of gap pages is written, a sub-agent independently re-scores **20%**
(minimum 3 — here, all 4) blind, per the method above. *(Library: P-07 Independent
Verification.)*

Pass threshold: **90%** of sampled pages match on the fear-acknowledging verdict
**and** the CTA pass/fail **and** voice within ±1. Below 90% → the voice/CTA
judgement is miscalibrated; recalibrate against the named quotes and the help-first
examples, and re-score before anything publishes.

---

## Worked check (the confiscation page)

A blind re-scorer sees the opening built on Muze Gu's words and should: A1 — confirm
the fear traces to the cited Facebook quote (not invented); A2 — confirm it resolves
("here is exactly what the document must say") and does not just frighten; A3 —
confirm the titer requirement is C-ID-cited and the rejection/release step is hedged
where MOCCAE is silent; A5 — confirm the checklist CTA beats "get a quote." Land:
voice 5/5, CTA pass, editorial 47/50 → **agree PUBLISH.** If a re-scorer marks the
opening voice 3 because they read it as generic, that is a legitimate split →
reconcile against the actual Muze Gu language before shipping, don't average to 4.

---

## What downgrades / forces a rewrite

- An opening whose fear can't be traced to a named quote or Column K fear.
- Any fear raised but not resolved in the same breath (the P-04 breach).
- A number with no C-ID and no honest hedge.
- A voice score ≥4 with no reason, or on brochure-voice copy.
- A CTA that fails the help-first test waved through.
- The independent re-score disagreeing on acknowledging-vs-exploiting.

---

## Output of the verification phase

Every gap page carries a reasoned voice score, a CTA-test result, a /50 editorial
score, and a complete C-ID source map; each has survived a blind re-score that
agrees it is fear-acknowledging and help-first; the batch audit logs ≥90%
agreement. That verified discipline is what makes File 04's automation safe — the
engine can draft and pre-score, but the publish gate is trusted only because the
fear-acknowledging judgement behind it is reproducible.
