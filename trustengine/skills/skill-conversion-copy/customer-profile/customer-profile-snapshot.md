## Why this matters for conversion copy

Conversion copy is built from two things and nothing else: **the reader's named
fear, in their own words** (Move 1) and **the verified fact that resolves it** (Move
2), closed with **an offer this buyer actually wants** (Move 3). This snapshot is
exactly that raw material — the verbatim language, the brand voice the words must
match, and the cited figures the copy is allowed to use. Write nothing you can't
trace to this page or the Source Bank.

---

## The reader → Move 1 (name the fear in their words)

The customer is **not buying transport — they are buying certainty, compliance,
safety, and emotional relief.** They arrive afraid, not curious. The opening must
sound like their situation, not a brochure.

| Persona | The fear the copy must name | Page it anchors |
|---------|----------------------------|-----------------|
| Import-to-Dubai owner | Confiscation at the airport | "dog taken at the airport" |
| Last-Minute Mover | Running out of time before summer | "move a pet in summer" |
| Cost-anxious owner | Being overcharged with no transparency | "titer test cost" |
| Confused Researcher | Overwhelm — doesn't know which airport/airline | "Sharjah vs Dubai vs Abu Dhabi" |

## Verbatim reader language (the gold for openings)

Copy that uses this language scores high on voice; copy that paraphrases it scores
low. Use it lightly cleaned, with attribution in the source map.

| Quote | Who / where | Fear it carries |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| *"without it your dog will be taken away in airport and never give back… Still when I remember I crying"* | Muze Gu, Facebook "Dog Lovers In UAE" — deepest fear in the dataset | Confiscation |
| *"shamelessly charging an insane amount of money"* / *"Please don't leave your pets behind"* | IrbisKat, Reddit r/dubai — 24↑ (highest in dataset) | Price gouging + abandonment |
| *"being quoted endless amount"* | 7Ssisi, Reddit | Titer-cost price gouging |

| *"every website says something different — I don't know who to trust"* | research synthesis | Overwhelm / distrust |

The 9 Column K fear categories these map to: Rejection/denial · Documentation
mistake · Airline rejection · Financial shock · Separation · Wrong provider · Time
pressure · Pet suffering · Process overwhelm.

## The brand voice → every line must be brand-aligned

**Core emotional promise:** *"Your dog is not cargo. Your dog is family. We manage
the process so you know what needs to happen, when, and how your dog gets there
safely."*
**What this brand is NOT** (rewrite if the copy sounds like these): a cargo company
· a generic freight service · a budget transport provider · a hype-merchant.
**Voice values:** Safety · Trust · Reliability · Professionalism · Communication ·
Expertise — warm, expert, calm. Never hyped, never salesy, never a manufactured
countdown.

## The verified facts the copy may cite → Move 2

Maximum-risk niche: every number is cited to a Verified Source Bank row **or honestly
hedged**. The honesty is the conversion move for this buyer.

| Fact | Status | Use in copy |
|------|--------|-------------|
| MOCCAE release fee **500 AED/dog** | Verified (C-003) | a real, checkable number |
| Permit valid **90 days** | Verified (C-010) | timeline certainty |
| Etihad pet fee **USD 399** (official) vs **USD 1,500** (community) | Verified conflict (C-015) | name the conflict, don't hide it |
| Rabies titer cost | **No official figure** — community **700–1,300 AED** | HEDGE: "no official price; here's the real range — get it in writing" |
| Summer embargo | seasonal airline restriction (verify per airline) | a *real* deadline — legitimate scarcity |

## What "help-first" means to this buyer → Move 3

This reader is frightened and has been quoted "endless amounts." The CTA that
converts gives them the thing that lowers the fear *before* asking for anything: the
airport checklist, the sourced cost breakdown, the embargo calendar, the routing
table. "Get a quote" reminds them of being overcharged — it raises the fear it
should lower. Offer first; the enquiry follows the help.

---

## How to use this snapshot

Writing a page: pick the persona + verbatim quote → write Move 1 (name the fear);
pick the verified fact/hedge → write Move 2 (resolve it); pick the matching offer →
write Move 3 (help-first CTA); check every line against the brand voice. Everything
else from the master profile is unnecessary for writing the copy.
