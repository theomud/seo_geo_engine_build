## PHASE 1 — MARKET RESEARCH ✅ DONE

**What was researched:** why high-fear service leads don't buy on day one and how the gap between enquiry and decision leaks; the two default failures (silence vs the sales blast); the psychology of fear appeals (Protection Motivation Theory); the published nurture canon (7-email frameworks, the AIM welcome arc, mid-week timing, one-CTA-per-email).
**Key findings:** the lead is won by staying *usefully present* — resolving one real fear per email with a verified, sourced answer — not by selling. A fear appeal only produces action when the threat is paired with a credible coping step (M-20); fear without a path produces avoidance. The sequence is the only asset that compounds without ongoing human involvement (Automation 5/5).
**Sources / library:** M-02 Fear Hierarchy, M-20 Protection Motivation Theory, M-21 Cialdini, M-32 Hierarchy of Effects; F-22 7-Email Nurture, F-23 AIM, F-18 AIDA, F-32 Funnel-Stage Copy Matching; P-19 80/20 Value-to-Pitch, P-20 Mid-Week Timing, P-21 One Primary CTA, P-04 Fear-Acknowledging, P-05 One Fear Per Email, P-15 Deliver Then Document.
**Verdict:** highest-leverage gap in the funnel; fully automatable after a one-time human write; highly teachable.

## WHAT THIS SKILL PROVED

**Core finding:** a nurture sequence's quality is objective and countable — every email must name a real, attributable fear, carry a verified C-ID (or an anchored honest hedge), and end help-first; 7/7 across the three gates is the bar.
**The counter-intuitive result:** in a market whose deepest practical fear is being overcharged, the **honest hedge out-converts the confident number** — naming an absence ("no official price — get it in writing") and a source conflict (C-015) builds more trust than asserting a figure.
**What others don't do:** generic nurture sells from email one and invents reassurance; this sequence gives six emails of verified, sourced help before a single ask, and its only deadline (the summer embargo + 90-day permit) is a real external one, never a manufactured countdown.
