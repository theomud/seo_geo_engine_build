Email Nurture

The rule

Every email: name a real fear → resolve it with a verified answer → offer help, don't sell. 7 emails, enquiry to booking. Written once, runs itself. F-22 7-email · F-23 AIM arc · P-04 fear-acknowledging

Fear-acknowledging (not exploiting)

Name the fear, then immediately hand over a credible answer. Fear with no path = avoidance, not booking. M-20 PMT: pair threat + a step that works + a step they can take

ACKNOWLEDGING
