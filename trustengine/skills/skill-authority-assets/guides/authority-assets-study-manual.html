Authority Asset Creation

The documented resource AI cannot replicate and competitors cannot copy — because they did not do the work. Real case, real failure, real proof, beside every claim.

Why this skill

Type "write a definitive guide to moving a pet to Dubai" into any AI and you get a competent, complete, useless page — the same one every competitor generated from the same prompt, trained on the same averages. It ranks nowhere and earns no links, because nothing in it could only have come from someone who did the work.

"Every website says something different — I don't know who to trust." — the reader who has read ten generic guides and believes none of them.
The governing principle is Hormozi's: documentation over creation. You don't invent a guide — you document what actually happened, with proof beside every claim. Delivery becomes marketing.

Document, don't create

The loop: do real work → document it with proof → surface the failures → publish → it attracts the next client → repeat. F-07 The work comes first; documenting work that didn't happen is fabrication. P-15

The Hormozi test

One question decides whether an asset is authority or mush: could a basic AI prompt reproduce this? P-13 You verify it by actually running the prompt and listing what your case has that the generic version can't.

The AI foil
"ensure all documentation is in order, including the rabies titer, to avoid your pet being held."
The documented case
the C-019/C-003/C-010 citations · the no-official-price hedge (C-001) · the turnaround that wasn't real · the one-day-to-spare outcome

The AI can't produce the right column — it has no access to the sources, the failure, or the lived margin. That gap is the asset.

Proof density

"Authority" is countable: ≥1 verifiable proof item per 200 words M-13 — a C-ID citation, a named figure, a dated fact, a documented failure. Under that, it's prose an AI could generate; over it, it's mostly evidence. The count is run by regex, not eyeballed.

The five beats

A documented case is structured in five beats, the fourth being the one that matters most:

BeatCarries
1 · The fearthe named fear in real customer words
2 · What happenedthe real situation, dated, specific
3 · The proofdocuments + figures, each cited by C-ID
4 · The failurethe surprise no AI can fabricate
5 · The outcomewhat actually resulted, not a hypothetical

The free-work bootstrap

Where the cases come from before paying clients exist: do real work in public — a free audit, a free walk-through — document every step, publish it as a case study. F-14 Each documented piece becomes an asset and attracts the next case. The loop runs itself.

Keeping it un-fakeable

The failure mode is an asset that looks authoritative but is dressed-up mush. Three defences:

  • A real failure must be documented — no surprise = a hypothetical = fails the Hormozi test.
  • Re-count proof density from the text — the regex count must reproduce ≥1 per 200 words.
  • Re-run the Hormozi test for real — generate the generic version; if it covers everything the asset says, the asset is mush.

A missing failure or an asset a generic prompt reproduces is a hard fail — no density score rescues it.
