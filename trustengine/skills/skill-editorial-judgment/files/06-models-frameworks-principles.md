## MODELS — the data structures and mental models this skill uses

- **M-10 — Ten-Criteria Quality Model.** Score every draft /50 across True, Specific, Useful, Clear, Commercial, Trustworthy, Better-than-competitors, Brand-aligned, Right-buyer, Publishable. *Source:* aligns with Google QRG "Page Quality" rubric.
- **M-11 — Content Depth Model.** Judges whether a page is comprehensive enough for its intent. *Source:* "comprehensive content" pillar (Nielsen 1999).
- **M-01 — Verified Fact Model.** The "True" criterion: every fact verified, no hallucinations, statistics sourced. *Source:* Google QRG factual-accuracy requirement.

## FRAMEWORKS — the decision systems and processes

- **F-06 — Seven-Step Editorial Process.** Remove the 7 weak patterns, then add specifics/proof/offer/objections/CTA. *Source:* consistent with Google QRG "Who/How/Why."
- **F-11 — Forty-Five-Check Audit.** The publishing checklist (Content / SEO / Conversion blocks). *Source:* maps onto NN/g's 53 trust recommendations.
- **F-31 — "Who / How / Why" Self-Assessment Loop.** The pre-publish question set behind the "would you publish this?" test. *Source:* Google Search Central.

## PRINCIPLES — the non-negotiable rules

- **P-13 ⟳ — Hormozi Test Before Publishing.** "Would you put your name on it?" *Update:* paired with Ogilvy's "no substitute for homework" and the AICPBSAWN framework so it isn't single-author. *Source:* Hormozi; Ogilvy.
- **P-11 — Real Examples Mandatory.** Replace every generic claim with a real number, name, or example ("4.8 stars from 283 reviews," not "many happy customers"). *Source:* Ogilvy.
- **P-03 — Proof Over Promise.** Show, don't tell — every claim carries proof or is cut. *Source:* Fogg et al. (2003).
