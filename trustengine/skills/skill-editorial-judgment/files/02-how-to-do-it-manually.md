# Skill · File 02 — How To Do It Manually
## Scoring a draft, removing the weak patterns, and deciding publish or hold

---

## Why manual first

The score is a judgement. Automation can flag the 7 weak patterns and suggest fixes, but "is this true / brand-aligned / strong enough to publish" stays human. Edit several pages by hand until the 10 criteria are automatic — then the checklist (File 04) makes the flagging fast and the human keeps the judgement.

Editing-time benchmarks: landing page (500w) 30–60 min · article (1,500w) 60–90 min · service page (800w) 45–75 min · FAQ (10 Qs) 30–45 min. Quality first, speed second.

---

## Step 1 — Score the draft on the 10 criteria (out of 50)

Score each criterion /5, against the page in front of you — not a vibe. *(Library: M-10 Ten-Criteria Quality Model.)*

| # | Criterion | Scores 5 when… |
|---|-----------|----------------|
| 1 | True | every fact verified or hedged; no hallucination *(M-01)* |
| 2 | Specific | real numbers/names/examples — could not apply to any business *(P-11)* |
| 3 | Useful | answers the reader's actual question; solves a real problem |
| 4 | Clear | scannable; main point obvious in 10 seconds |
| 5 | Commercial | supports a goal; one clear next step |
| 6 | Trustworthy | proof for every claim; honest not hyped *(P-03)* |
| 7 | Better than competitors | more specific/comprehensive/credible than the top page |
| 8 | Brand-aligned | the owner would recognise their voice |
| 9 | Right buyer | speaks their situation, language, decision stage |
| 10 | Strong enough to publish | would you put your name on it? *(P-13 Hormozi Test)* |

**Publish gate: 40+/50** and no single criterion below 3.

---

## Step 2 — Remove the 7 weak patterns

Find and cut every instance — name the pattern, apply the fix: *(Library: F-06 Seven-Step Editorial Process.)*

| Pattern | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| 1 Filler Opening ("In today's fast-paced world…") | open with the reader's problem or a specific fact |
| 2 Laundry List ("services include A,B,C and much more") | prioritise and describe |
| 3 Unsupported Claim ("the leading provider") | show, don't tell — data/proof |
| 4 Circular Definition ("quality ensures excellence") | delete or make specific |
| 5 Vague Benefit ("innovative solutions") | which solution? which result? |
| 6 Fake Enthusiasm ("passionate about exceptional results!") | show passion through specifics |
| 7 Dead End ("thanks for visiting!") | end with a clear next step |

---

## Step 3 — Add what's missing

After cutting, add the seven strengtheners: **specifics** (a real number/name for every generic claim), **proof** (citations, screenshots, real testimonials), a **clear offer**, **buyer objections** answered, **local details**, **concrete examples**, and a **stronger CTA** (replace "Contact us" with something specific and helpful).

---

## Step 4 — Re-score and decide

Re-run the 10 criteria. **40+/50 and nothing below 3 → publish.** Below that → hold, and the edit notes say exactly what must change. A page that can't clear 40 is not "nearly there" — it is held.

---

## Step 5 — Record the edit notes

Write what changed and why (e.g. "removed Pattern 3 unsupported 'best in Dubai'; added the 4.8★/283-reviews figure; CTA changed from 'Get a Quote' to 'Download the MOCCAE checklist'"). The notes make the /50 reproducible and the decision auditable.

---

## The publishing checklist (nothing ships without it)

**Content:** True · Specific · Useful · Clear · Commercial · Trustworthy · Better-than-competitors · Brand-aligned · Buyer-focused · Publishable.
**SEO:** title 50–60 chars w/ keyword · meta 150–160 · H1 has primary keyword · keyword in first paragraph · internal links present.
**Conversion:** CTA clear & prominent · contact info correct · trust signals visible · objections addressed. *(Library: F-11 maps onto NN/g's 53 trust recommendations.)*

## Worked example — scoring the airport-confiscation gap page

| Criterion | Score | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| True | 5 | every fact cites a Verified row (C-019/010/003) or is hedged (C-001) |
| Specific | 5 | real C-IDs, the 500 AED fee, Muze Gu's quote |
| Useful | 5 | answers the exact fear + the 3 prevention steps |
| Clear | 4 | 5-layer structure, scannable; one heading could be sharper |
| Commercial | 4 | help-first CTA; could add a second micro-conversion |
| Trustworthy | 5 | MOCCAE screenshot beside the claim |
| Better than competitors | 5 | 9/9 competitors never address confiscation |
| Brand-aligned | 4 | voice fits; confirm against the brand guide |
| Right buyer | 5 | opens in the Import-to-Dubai owner's words |
| Strong enough to publish | 5 | yes — name-on-it |
| **Total** | **47/50** | **PUBLISH** |

A competitor's confiscation page would not exist (they avoid the fear) — which is precisely the gap. *(The four gap-page scores are this skill's real output: `data/editorial-scorecards.md`.)*

---

## What you must not do

- **Do not call it proofreading.** You are deciding rank/convert/trust, not grammar.
- **Do not pass a generic claim.** Every "best/leading/competitive" needs a real number or it's cut.
- **Do not publish below 40/50** to hit a deadline — hold it.
- **Do not score on vibe.** Each criterion is judged against the page, with a reason in the edit notes.
- **Do not approve an unverified fact.** "True" requires a citation or an honest hedge.

---

## Output of this manual phase

Each draft has a /50 scorecard, edit notes, and a publish/hold decision; the four universal-gap pages are scored end-to-end. That scorecard set is the real output and the input to File 04's automation — the engine flags patterns and pre-scores, the human still owns the publish call.
