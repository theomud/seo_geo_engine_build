## WHAT THIS SKILL PROVED

**Core finding:** a /50 rubric with anti-leniency caps turns "is this good?" into an objective, auditable decision — and it catches real defects a vibe-read ships (the "cabin-... cargo" typo). Specific and Trustworthy are where AI content dies; the rubric makes that visible and fixable.
**What changed from the spec:** the score is the *content* /50; the SEO block of the publishing checklist is finalised at publish time (a mechanical step), so a page can be content-approved while SEO meta is pending.
**What others don't do:** no off-the-shelf QC ties "True" to a verified source bank by C-ID or caps the score for uncited/generic claims in a regulated market.
