The 7 weak patterns — cut every time

  1. Filler Opening ("in today's fast-paced world")
  2. Laundry List ("A, B, C and much more")
  3. Unsupported Claim ("the leading provider")
  4. Circular Definition ("quality ensures excellence")
  5. Vague Benefit ("innovative solutions")
  6. Fake Enthusiasm ("passionate about results!")
  7. Dead End ("contact us for a quote")
