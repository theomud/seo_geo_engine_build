## The 10-Criteria Quality Framework

Score every piece of content on all 10 before publishing.

**1. TRUE**
All facts verified. No AI hallucinations. Statistics have sources.

**2. SPECIFIC**
Contains real numbers, names, examples. Could not apply to any business.
"We have many happy customers" → fails.
"4.8 stars from 283 Google reviews" → passes.

**3. USEFUL**
Answers the reader's actual question. Provides actionable information.
Saves the reader time. Solves a real problem.

**4. CLEAR**
Easy to scan. Logical structure. Short paragraphs. Descriptive headers.
Main point is obvious within 10 seconds.

**5. COMMERCIAL**
Supports a business goal. Clear next step for the reader.
Content without a purpose is a waste of resources.

**6. TRUSTWORTHY**
Honest, not hyped. Proof for every claim. Real testimonials.
No red flags. Professional but human.

**7. BETTER THAN COMPETITORS**
More specific, more comprehensive, more useful, more credible,
more original than the current top-ranking pages.

**8. BRAND-ALIGNED**
Sounds like this specific business. The owner would recognise
their voice. Correct terminology. Feels authentic.

**9. WRITTEN FOR THE RIGHT BUYER**
Addresses their specific situation. Speaks their language.
Matches their knowledge level. Fits their decision stage.

**10. STRONG ENOUGH TO PUBLISH**
Would you put your name on this? Would you show it to a client?
If not — what specifically needs to change?

## The 7 Patterns of Weak AI Content — Remove Every Time

**Pattern 1: The Filler Opening**
"In today's fast-paced world, [topic] has become increasingly important."
Fix: Start with the reader's problem or a compelling specific fact.

**Pattern 2: The Laundry List**
"Our services include A, B, C, D, E, F, and much more."
Fix: Prioritise and describe. No list without context.

**Pattern 3: The Unsupported Claim**
"We are the leading provider in the industry."
Fix: Show, don't tell. Use testimonials, data, proof.

**Pattern 4: The Circular Definition**
"Quality is important because it ensures excellence."
Fix: Delete or replace with a specific, meaningful statement.

**Pattern 5: The Vague Benefit**
"Our innovative solutions help businesses achieve their goals."
Fix: What solutions? Which businesses? What specific goals?

**Pattern 6: The Fake Enthusiasm**
"We are passionate about delivering exceptional results!"
Fix: Real passion is shown through specifics, not stated.

**Pattern 7: The Dead End**
"Thank you for visiting our website. Have a nice day!"
Fix: Every page ends with a clear, specific next step.

## What To Add After Removing Weak Content

**Specifics:** Replace every generic claim with a real number, name, or example.
**Proof:** Add testimonials, case studies, credentials, data.
**Clear offer:** Make it obvious what is being sold and what is included.
**Buyer objections:** Address concerns before they become reasons not to buy.
**Local details:** For local businesses, name neighbourhoods, landmarks, area.
**Concrete examples:** Real scenarios, not abstract descriptions.
**Stronger CTAs:** Replace "Contact us" with something specific and helpful.

## The Publishing Checklist

Nothing ships without passing this.

CONTENT:
- [ ] True (facts verified)
- [ ] Specific (numbers, names, examples)
- [ ] Useful (answers questions, solves problems)
- [ ] Clear (scannable, readable, logical)
- [ ] Commercial (supports business goals)
- [ ] Trustworthy (proof, honesty, credibility)
- [ ] Better than competitors
- [ ] Brand-aligned (correct voice and tone)
- [ ] Buyer-focused (right audience, right concerns)
- [ ] Publishable (no obvious flaws)

SEO:
- [ ] Title tag 50-60 characters with keyword
- [ ] Meta description 150-160 characters
- [ ] H1 includes primary keyword
- [ ] Keyword in first paragraph
- [ ] Internal links present

CONVERSION:
- [ ] CTA is clear and prominent
- [ ] Contact info correct
- [ ] Trust signals visible
- [ ] Objections addressed

## Editing Time Benchmarks

Landing page (500 words): 30-60 minutes
Article (1,500 words): 60-90 minutes
Service page (800 words): 45-75 minutes
FAQ section (10 questions): 30-45 minutes

Speed improves with practice. Focus on quality first, speed second.
