# Can I move my pet to Dubai in summer? The heat-embargo months and how to plan around them

## Layer 1 — Fear acknowledgement
If you are moving in June, July or August and have just heard there is a "summer ban" on flying pets, the worry is real: people discover the embargo *after* they have booked, and then scramble. This page explains which months are affected, why, and exactly how to plan a summer move so your pet still travels safely.

## Layer 2 — The verified answer (and an honest gap)
Two different things govern a summer move, and it matters which is which:

- **The regulator (MOCCAE) requirements do not change by season.** The import permit is still applied for online and is valid 90 days. *(Verified — Source Bank C-019, C-010.)*
- **The heat embargo is an airline/cargo rule, not a MOCCAE rule.** Pets into Dubai travel as cargo, not in the cabin — *"no animals in the cabin; pets travel via flydubai Cargo only."* *(Verified — flydubai; Source Bank C-022.)* Cargo operators apply temperature limits in peak summer.

> **Honest gap:** the exact embargo dates and temperature thresholds are **not published by MOCCAE** and vary by airline and route. No official government figure exists — treat the commonly-cited "June–September" window as airline/community guidance and confirm the specific dates with your chosen carrier's live cargo policy before booking. *(Unverifiable by official source — flagged, not asserted.)*

## Layer 3 — Process (the summer plan)
1. **Start early.** Build the rabies + permit timeline so nothing forces a peak-heat flight (permit valid 90 days — C-010).
2. **Confirm the carrier's live heat policy** for your route and dates (cargo-only into Dubai — C-022).
3. **If your window falls inside the embargo,** plan a night flight, a cooler shoulder-date, or a routing the carrier still accepts.
4. **Have the MOCCAE permit issued online and timed to the flight** (C-019).
