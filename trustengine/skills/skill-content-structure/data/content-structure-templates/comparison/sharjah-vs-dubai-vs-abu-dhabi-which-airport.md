# Sharjah vs Dubai vs Abu Dhabi — which airport for moving your pet?

## Layer 1 — Fear acknowledgement
Choosing the wrong airport — or the wrong process at the right one — is where documentation mistakes happen. If you are trying to work out whether Sharjah, Dubai, or Abu Dhabi is the simplest route for your pet, this page lays out what is the same at all three (the regulator's rules) and what genuinely differs (the route experience).

## Layer 2 — The verified answer (what's identical everywhere)
The MOCCAE rules apply to **all** UAE entry points — the airport you pick does not change the regulator's requirements:
- **The import permit is applied for online, before travel, regardless of airport.** *(Verified — Source Bank C-019.)*
- **Pets travel as cargo, not cabin** (e.g. flydubai: *"no animals in the cabin… via flydubai Cargo only"*). *(Verified — Source Bank C-022.)*

So a "shortcut airport" never bypasses the permit or the rabies timeline.
