# What happens if your dog is taken at Dubai airport — and the 3 steps that prevent it

## Layer 1 — Fear acknowledgement
If you have read that your dog could be taken at Dubai airport and never given back, that fear is real and it has happened to people. One owner in a UAE pet group wrote: *"without it your dog will be taken away in airport and never give back from you my friends had the worst experience. Still when I remember I crying."* This page explains exactly what triggers a hold at the border, what it costs to get your dog released, and the three documented steps that prevent it.

## Layer 2 — The verified answer
A pet is held at the entry point when its paperwork is incomplete — most often a missing import permit or an invalid rabies record. The good news: the requirements are published by the regulator, MOCCAE, and they are specific.

- **You apply for the import permit online, before travel.** *(Verified — MOCCAE import-of-pets page; Source Bank C-019, verified 2026-05-28.)*
- **The permit is valid for 90 days from issuance** — so time it to your flight. *(Verified — Source Bank C-010.)*
- **The rabies vaccination is only valid at least 21 days after the first vaccination** — vaccinate in good time. *(Verified — Source Bank C-007.)*
- **If a dog is held, the published release fee is 500 AED per dog (250 AED per cat)** at the Cargo Village. *(Verified — MOCCAE; Source Bank C-003.)*

> On the titer-test cost specifically: **MOCCAE publishes no figure.** Community reports put it in the 700–1,300 AED range — treat that as community-sourced, not official, and confirm with the lab. *(Unverifiable — Source Bank C-001.)*
