Content Structure for Trust

5 layers · 9 page types · cite every claim by C-ID

The 5 layers — every page

1 · Fear acknowledgement (first 100 words) — the fear in their words."…your dog will be taken away in airport and never give back" — Muze Gu
2 · Verified answer — every claim cites a Verified row."permit valid 90 days" Verified C-010 · "release 500 AED/dog" C-003
3 · Process / evidence — official screenshot beside the claim.MOCCAE page embedded = proof (silence on titer cost = proof too, C-001)
4 · Related fears — link 2–3 neighbour pages.confiscation → checklist · titer cost · summer embargo
5 · Help-first CTA — useful, not a sales ask."Download the MOCCAE-verified checklist" not "Get a Quote"

The 9 page types

Fear Resolution Fear → confiscation
Process Guide Informational → overwhelm
Cost Transparency Commercial → price gouging
Comparison Research → wrong choice
Route/Variant Commercial → doc mistake
Urgency Urgency → deadline
Emergency Emergency → crisis now
Case Study Problem → "works for me?"
Trust Page Research → "trust them?"

The opening formula

[Fear in their words] + [reassurance bridge] + [what this page gives them]"If you've been told your dog could be taken at Dubai airport — that fear is real, and it has happened. This page explains what triggers it and the 3 steps that prevent it."

Source Bank — 3 handlings

Verified → assert it + cite C-ID."valid 90 days" C-010
Unverifiable → hedge, never assert.titer cost: "no official figure; community 700–1,300 AED" C-001
Conflicting → lead with official, name it.Etihad: official USD 399, not the community's USD 1,500 — C-015

The Hormozi 3 questions

1 · Would you put your name on it?
2 · Is the proof undeniable & real-world? (dated official screenshot)
3 · Could AI alone have written it? If yes → add real specifics.

Proof density rule

Higher risk = more proof. Dubai pet relocation = maximum risk → every claim traces to an official source or is openly hedged. Proof goes beside each claim, never one testimonials block.
