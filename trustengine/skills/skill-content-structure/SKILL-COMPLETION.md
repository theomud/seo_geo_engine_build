## PHASE 1 — MARKET RESEARCH ✅ DONE

**What was researched:**
- [x] How standard content frameworks organise (around topics/keywords)
- [x] Published trust/credibility models (NN/g Hierarchy of Trust, Nielsen credibility factors)
- [x] Protection Motivation Theory (the fear-appeal basis)
- [x] Hormozi proof-interstitial thinking

**Key findings:** standard frameworks organise around the *topic*; this skill organises around the *fear behind the topic*. It is a structural system (5-layer page, 9 page types, Intent × Fear matrix), not a writing style. No published framework combines fear-acknowledging opening + verified-claim citation + proof interstitial + one-fear-per-page into a fixed architecture.

**Sources reviewed:**
| Source | Insight (MFP code) |
|--------|--------------------|
| NN/g Hierarchy of Trust (Aurora Harley) | 5 commitment levels — parent of the 5-layer page (M-18) |
| Nielsen 4 credibility factors | why Layer-3 evidence must be real (M-19) |
| Protection Motivation Theory (Rogers 1975) | one threat-coping pair per appeal → one fear per page (M-20/P-05) |
| Fogg et al. 2003 / Google QRG | proof beside claims — Proof Interstitial (F-08/P-03) |

**Verdict:** novel + improved. Documented in files/01 and files/06.

Plus `data/content-structure-briefs.md` (the register of all four) and a rendered page-1 HTML.

**What failed or surprised:**
- **Hedging the unpublished is the honesty advantage, not a weakness.** Two of the four pages turn on Unverifiable data (titer cost C-001; summer-embargo dates) — written as "no official figure exists," they are *stronger* than competitors who assert a number.
- **Silence is proof.** The cost page embeds the full MOCCAE page to show it says nothing about the titer cost — the absence is the evidence that any "official price" is not official.
- **A real conflict surfaced** — Etihad's official USD 399 vs the community's USD 1,500 (C-015) — exactly the customer-protecting finding competitors miss.

**Verdict:** the structure works end-to-end on real data. One spec change confirmed: the Source Bank is consumed **Verified-only** for assertions; Unverifiable rows are passed in to *hedge*, never assert.

## WHAT THIS SKILL PROVED

**The core finding:** a fear-first opening + Verified-cited claims + honest hedging of the unpublished + proof beside each claim is exactly what the market (competitors avg 3.9/10) misses. The structure converts the upstream fear and source research into pages that earn trust in sequence.

**What changed from the original spec:** Source Bank consumed Verified-only for assertions (Unverifiable → hedge); the .xlsx brief output was realised as a .md register for the manual run.

**What competitors do not do:** no competitor opens with the customer's verbatim fear, cites claims by official source, or hedges the unpublished honestly — they assert numbers no authority publishes.
