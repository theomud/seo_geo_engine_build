## Why this matters for Customer Fear Intelligence

This skill converts keywords into intent + a verbatim fear statement. To do that, you need the customer's emotional state and language — not demographics. The core insight: the customer is **not buying transport — they are buying certainty, compliance, safety, and emotional relief.** Underneath a practical search ("cost", "documents", "timeline") is almost always a fear: *"Will my dog be safe, accepted, and able to arrive with us?"* The Fear Formula ("I'm afraid that…") names that fear in their own words.

## Search behaviour → intent classification

Keywords cluster into three journey stages, which drive the intent label (Column J):
- **Early (Informational):** "How to move a dog to Dubai", "Dubai pet import rules", "pet relocation Dubai requirements" — wants to understand.
- **Mid (Commercial / Research):** "best pet relocation company Dubai", "Blue Sky reviews", route terms like "pet relocation Dubai to India" — comparing/deciding.
- **Acute (Fear / Urgency / Emergency):** "is it safe to fly a dog to Dubai", "emergency pet relocation Dubai", "pet quarantine Dubai" — a worry or deadline blocks progress.

Decision order when classifying: Emergency → Urgency → Fear → Problem → Research/brand → Commercial → Transactional → default Informational.

## Real community language (verbatim — never invent a fear)

- "Been quoted some ridiculous prices from companies and vets"
- "Every website says something different — I don't know who to trust"
- "relocation companies are shamelessly charging an insane amount of money" (IrbisKat, 24 upvotes)
- "Please do proper research and read reviews before handing over your pet to them" (Curious_cat_2912, 16 upvotes)

Every fear statement must be matched to documented language like this — specific and visceral, never generic ("I'm worried about the process" is rejected).

## How to use this when mapping fears

For each keyword: assign intent from the search-behaviour stage, then complete "I'm afraid that…" using the emotional driver and the verbatim community language for that fear family. A keyword like "pet relocation Dubai to India" is not an information request — it is *"I'm afraid the paperwork for India has a mistake that gets my dog rejected at the border."* That fear is what the downstream page must open with.
