Intent Decision Tree

Ask in order. First YES wins → stop. Fills Column J.

The 7 questions

Q1 · Emergency? → Emergency
stuck at airport · flight tomorrow/today · right now · stranded · pet held
Q2 · Urgency? → Urgency
before summer · before the embargo · this month · by [date] · deadline
Q3 · Fear? → Fear
safe · risk · can · will · what if · denied · quarantine · confiscated
Q4 · Problem? → Problem
failed · rejected · not accepted · "what to do if" · "how to fix" · next steps
Q5 · Brand / named resource? → Research
Blue Sky · DKC · Emirates · Etihad · MOCCAE · IPATA · a named form
Q6 · Commercial? → Commercial
best · top · compare · vs · review · cost · price · quote · company
Q7 · Transactional? → Transactional
book · hire · contact · get a quote · near me · company + location
No signal? → INFORMATIONAL
Urgency vs Emergency: deadline in days/weeks = Urgency; crisis in hours = Emergency

Edge-case rules

"cost" w/o "quote/get" → Informational; with buy context → Commercial
Route, no question word → default Commercial
Fear + commercial ("safest company") → Fear wins
Brand + "reviews" → Research (evaluating, not deciding)
Always hand-review every Emergency, Urgency, Fear & Problem call and every brand keyword. ~80% auto-classify; 20% need eyes. The Trust Engine · Customer Fear Intelligence · File 02 · 8 intent types (updated 2026-05-29).
