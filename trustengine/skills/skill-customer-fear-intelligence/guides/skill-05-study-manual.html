Volume Validation

Checking real search volume for every validated keyword — and using it correctly.

Volume is a priority signal, not a value signal

The engine tells you what people search. Volume tells you how often. Crucially, that determines content priority — not content value.

A keyword with 10 monthly searches can be worth more than one with 1,000 — if those 10 are people with a confirmed move date and a dog that needs relocating. Never confuse frequency with worth.

The zero-volume rule

A keyword with zero Keyword Planner volume is not automatically rejected. Planner significantly undercounts niche markets. Keep a zero-volume keyword if any of these hold:

Appeared in 3+ sources during collection
Contains a specific route / location / fear combination
Matches real community language from Facebook or Reddit
Is a question-format fear keyword

Always document the reasoning in the Notes column. Recording rules: exact number if available (e.g. 1,300), < 10 if very low, not found if no data — and never delete a keyword for low/zero volume.

The Authority Hacker case study

Authority Hacker documented a case where their #1 ranking keyword delivered a fraction of the traffic Keyword Planner predicted — and a "zero-volume" keyword outperformed it.

The lesson: Planner is an estimate built for big-budget advertisers, not niche regulated markets. Treat its numbers as a rough priority hint, never as a gate on whether a real customer exists.

Seasonal adjustment — Column H

The Dubai summer cargo embargo splits the year into two content seasons:

Oct–Apr — active season
Cooler months, airlines carry pet cargo. Cargo / transport keywords → "Oct–Apr". Commercial and route pages convert now.
Jun–Sep — embargo season
Most airlines suspend pet cargo. Regulation / document keywords → "Year-round". Publish evergreen guides that rank in time for the October surge.

Urgency keywords spike in April/May — the week before the embargo, people panic ("can I still move my dog before summer Dubai"). That deserves its own urgent-intent page.
