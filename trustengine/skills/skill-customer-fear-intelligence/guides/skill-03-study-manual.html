The Fear Formula

What fear mapping is — and why no competitor does it

Intent tells you what someone wants to find. Fear tells you what they're afraid of. They're different things. Someone searching "pet relocation Dubai to India" wants a service — but they're afraid their dog will be denied entry at the Indian border over a paperwork mistake they didn't catch.

Generic page (loses)

"Professional pet relocation from Dubai to India."

Fear-acknowledging page (wins)

"We handle every document so your dog arrives in India legally and safely — nothing rejected at the border."

This is the step nobody else does. The fear database below is built entirely from real quotes across 69 screenshots of Facebook groups and Reddit threads. We do not invent fear — we acknowledge a documented one and pair it with a verified solution.

How to write a fear statement

For every keyword in Column K, complete one sentence:

"I'm afraid that…"

It must be:

  • Specific — not "I'm worried about the process" but "I'm afraid the rabies titre timeline means my dog gets stuck in quarantine for 6 months."
  • Visceral — it should feel like something real could go wrong.
  • Tied to a real outcome — rejection at customs, separation, financial loss, pet injury, wasted time.
  • In the customer's exact words — the language from Facebook and Reddit, not formal copy.
  • Max 25 words after "I'm afraid that".
